**The call:** Every line whose advanced block describes a PLAYER (block offset 0 a `Player-` guid, its max health above 0) and carries a pool (offsets 10–12: power type, current, max — a cast spending two resources lists `a|b` and the first is kept; none at max 0) writes that player's 1 s series of that power type on the R12 grid from the segment's start: the LAST report of each second wins, the type keeps the largest max seen, a second no line reported is `None` — `Segment::power(player)` → `Vec<PowerSeries>`, one per type ascending, bounded by `MAX_BUCKETS`. Keyed by the raw guid, players only and never folded: a pet's pool is its own (R27). Passive: read ahead of the line's own event through the passive gate. Stored on the details tier, answered on `StoredFight.power` and `Breakdown.power` (v44).

**A conflicting change would:** Fold a pet's focus into its owner's pool, average a second's reports instead of keeping its last, fill a second nothing reported (a Druid in cat form reports energy, not mana) with a guess, or let a report open or extend a segment and break scanner/meter lockstep.

## Detail

THE FORMAT: the 19-field advanced block's offsets 10–12 (FORMAT-NOTES; the parser's `HpHint.power` since v43). It describes the unit the event is about — the source on a cast or a swing, the target on a damage or heal line — so a healer's own casts and the heals landing on them both report their mana, and a real raid night's 50 segments hold 1 064 player series (534 of them mana). A Druid's shapeshifts read as two series with gaps (mana in caster form, energy in cat form); `model::primary_power` names the type reported in the most seconds. GRID AND MERGE: bucket i covers [i·1000, (i+1)·1000) ms from the segment's start (21 600 at most); an Overall lays each member's seconds at its offset on the visit's clock (members never overlap; on the clamp the later member's report wins, as a later report of one second does). PASSIVE: the index scanner reads no advanced block, so the report is taken at the top of `Meter::feed` through `open_segment_for_passive` BEFORE the line's own event can open or extend a segment — the line that opens a trash pull reports into nothing, one past a pull's end or the trash gap lands nowhere, and a lazily loaded slice agrees (`tests/tree.rs`'s lazy picture carries every player's series on every fixture). STORE: each details player writes `power` (`{power_type, max, bucket_ms, per_sec: [n | null]}`; absent before v44 = empty), demoted with the rest of the details; `StoredFight.power` carries the drilled player's whatever the view, the stored `Breakdown.power` the same; SQL's `power` view (probed). LIVE: `Breakdown.power` for a `Window` session (`engine::wants_series`), whose inspector draws a HEALER's mana as a thin line under the graph (gui-logic's `inspect::power::healer_mana`). `check.awk` recomputes it on every fixture — per player the seconds reported (`power_seconds`), Σ of each second's last report (`power_sum`) and Σ of each type's largest max (`power_max`) — and the meter's `r28_*` tests pin the last-report rule, a type switch, a pet's pool, the gate and the Overall's offsets.


## Where it lives

The passive read at the top of `Meter::feed` and `Segment::power` are
[`wowdps-core`](../crates/core.md)'s; the series is
[`wowdps-model`](../crates/model.md)'s `PowerSeries` (with
`primary_power`); [`wowdps-proto`](../crates/proto.md) carries it on
`Breakdown.power`, `StoredFight.power` and the details tier, which
[`wowdps-daemon`](../crates/daemon.md) fills live for a `Window` session
and from the store for a drill; [`wowdps-history`](../crates/history.md)
unnests it as the probed `power` view; [`wowdps-mcp`](../crates/mcp.md)'s
`stored_fight { player }` answers it as `power`, the primary pool first; and
[`wowdps-gui`](../crates/gui.md) draws a healer's mana under the inspector's
graph (gui-logic's `inspect::power`). The pool's gains are [R27](r27.md)'s,
and like R27 it never folds a pet's pool onto its owner, unlike
[R5](r5.md)'s rule for every ledger of output; it is read where
[R23](r23.md) reads a sight of life, ahead of the line's event. Gated by
`check.awk`'s `power_seconds` / `power_sum` / `power_max` on every fixture
(the hand-worked values in [tree.txt](../fixtures/tree.md),
[support.txt](../fixtures/support.md) and [sample.txt](../fixtures/sample.md))
and the lazy-load picture in `tests/tree.rs`; the decision is
[A Pool, Second by Second](../decisions/a-pool-second-by-second.md).
