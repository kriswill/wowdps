**The call:** A player's Damage and Healing by-ability rows NEST, at READ time: a pet's (or guardian's) rows hang under the spell that SUMMONED it — its first SPELL_SUMMON, by the player or a unit of theirs, keyed by the pet's NAME as the rows are — or under the pet itself when no SUMMON was seen or the summon is a hunter's "Call Pet N"; a spell only one trinket grants (the generated item table's owner, `class_spells` vetoing first as R12's marks do) hangs under the item, as do a trinket-summoned pet's rows; a curated talent proc hangs under the spell that DRIVES it (step 4, v37: Blackened Soul under Wither); and every row splits into its `(spell id, periodic)` PARTS, which sum to it exactly. Each row also carries its CASTS: every `SPELL_CAST_SUCCESS` by the player and their pets under the row's name, through the passive gate. Step 3: its MISSES — every `*_MISSED` but an ABSORB (R1's hit) by the player and their pets under the row's name — and, on the player's own row, a DoT's UPTIME: the union of time their DEBUFF of that name is up on any enemy. Nothing opens, extends or splits a segment; lazy = full.

**A conflicting change would:** Let a pet's abilities scatter among the player's, lose a DoT's tick share inside one row, count a precast or a post-kill cast, or hang a class spell under a trinket — or nest a proc under a driver nothing in the client or the logs ties it to.

## Detail

GROUPS: `Segment::spell_tree(player, view)` → `SpellTree { groups, rows }`, empty for every view but Damage and Healing. A pet row ("spell\0pet") takes its group from the segment's `summons` — `(raw summoner guid, pet name) → (id, name)`, first SUMMON wins, kept on the `Meter` (seeded into every segment like `owners`, written into the latest segment too; every SUMMON is an index seed, so a lazy load agrees) and read for the summoners that fold onto the player, the lowest summon id winning when two of theirs summoned one name: `Summon` (`"summon:{spell name}"`, the spell's icon) — unless the summon spell is a trinket's (`Item`, below) or named "Call Pet …" (a stable's button, not the pet: `Pet`, `"pet:{pet name}"`, the spell's icon); no SUMMON seen → `Pet` with no icon (0 = the reader draws its first member's). A player's own row takes `Item` (`"item:{item id}"`, the item's name, no icon) when its first-seen spell id is no class's and one trinket owns it (`item_spells::trinket_of`, generated: each trinket's effect spells chased two EffectTriggerSpell levels, a spell kept only when every trinket reaching it bears ONE ItemSparse name — the lowest id of it; a stat proc a hundred trinkets share belongs to none; a spell the kind table files as a consumable is never a trinket's). Step 4 (v37): a player's own row still in no group whose parts carry a spell id the curated proc table names (`proc_spells::driver_of`, generated by `tools/gen-proc-spells.sh`: each entry tied to its driver by the client's own text — the proc's description or one it references names the driver, or references its id — or by the driver's SpellEffect triggering it, AND shown by the committed real-log census, `tools/census-proc-spells.sh`: at least 90% of the proc's direct onsets within 1.5 s after the same player's driver, or of its hits on a target carrying their driver aura) hangs under its DRIVER, found by NAME — the player's own row, else the first pet row wearing it (Blighted Maw rides the Dreadstalkers' Dreadbite): the proc joins the driver row's group when it has one, else the two form `Spell` (`"spell:{driver name}"`, the driver's name, the driver row's lowest spell id as icon); a proc whose driver dealt nothing in the segment stands alone. A proc with two drivers has no entry. A group of one is still a group on the wire; readers draw it as its row with the group's word after it. • PARTS: `SpellSlot::parts` splits every record — all views, so the invariant holds everywhere — by `(id, periodic)`: a Damage event's `periodic` (`_PERIODIC_` in the event name), a Heal's (`SPELL_PERIODIC_HEAL`), false for an absorb credit and every count view. `SpellMeta.parts` is sent only for a row with two or more, sorted by `(spell id, periodic)`; Σ amount / extra / count / crits = the row's. `Segment::periodic_amount(player, view)` = Σ the periodic parts. • CASTS: counted per raw caster guid per spell NAME in the segment the passive gate admits (`open_segment_for_passive`: an open segment not past the trash gap) for any caster the segment controls (`Player-` / `Pet-`, or one it knows an owner of) — so a precast before ENCOUNTER_START, a cast after the kill, one in the trash dead zone and a hostile NPC's land nowhere — and folded at read like `actors`. A row's casts join by name (a pet's by its pet name); a cast with no row under it (a summon, a buff) is in `Segment::casts(player)` and on no row. A swing or a proc has none. • MISSES AND UPTIME (step 3): a `*_MISSED` line other than an ABSORB (R1's hit, counted on the row) by a unit the segment controls, not its own target, counts once on the attacker's row by name (Miss % = misses / (hits + misses)); a player's `Debuff` on an enemy keeps a per-(player, debuff name) union over its targets — applied or refreshed adds one, removed drops one, time accruing while any holds it, one still open closing at read time — so a DoT on two enemies at once counts once; `Segment::misses_dealt` and `dot_uptime_ms` are the sums the fixture gates. • MERGE: an Overall's parts, casts and summons are its members' (summons first-seen in member order). • STORE (v36): the details tier keeps each player's `damage_tree` / `heal_tree` (`proto::history::spell_tree_json`; kinds by name, parts' `periodic` a bool); a details file written before v36 reads an empty tree and its abilities stand flat; the stored Damage / Healing drill answers with it. • Gated by `fixtures/tree.txt` (a Destruction Warlock: a Sayaad and an Infernal summoned mid-pull, Wither's hit and tick under two ids, Araz's Ritual Forge's two effects under one name, Eradicating Arcanocore; a Priest's Shadow Word: Pain hit and ticks under ONE id, Renew's instant heal and ticks, Flash Heal; a precast, a cast after the kill, a hostile cast, a cast before the trash's first hit and one in its dead zone) — `check.awk`'s `casts`, `damage_periodic` and `heal_periodic` on every fixture — and `crates/core/tests/tree.rs` (the groups, the parts, casts per row, the hunter's "Call Pet 1", every tree's shape on every fixture and Overall, lazy = full, the merge).

## Where it lives

The feed side is small and passive, in [`wowdps-core`](../crates/core.md)'s
`meter.rs`: every by-spell slot splits its tally into `(spell id, periodic)`
parts as it records (all views, so Σ parts = the row everywhere), casts count
through the same passive gate [R17](r17.md)'s misses and [R18](r18.md)'s
auras use, and a SPELL_SUMMON remembers its spell per (summoner, pet name) on
the `Meter` beside [R22](r22.md)'s `summoned` map. Casts never were combat, so
the index scanner has nothing new to mirror; summons are already index seeds,
so a lazy load agrees. The grouping happens at READ time in
`Segment::spell_tree`, over the same actor fold [R5](r5.md)'s pet rows use.

The trinket half leans on [R12](r12.md)'s generated item table, which now
also names each trinket spell's single owning item — [gen-item-spells](../tools/gen-item-spells.md)
reads ItemSparse for the names — with `class_spells` ([R8](r8.md)) vetoing
first, exactly as the R12 marks do.

The shape is [`wowdps-model`](../crates/model.md)'s `SpellTree`, a trailing
field of every `Breakdown` in [`wowdps-proto`](../crates/proto.md)
(`PROTO_VERSION` 36); [`wowdps-daemon`](../crates/daemon.md)'s engine fills it
on a Damage or Healing drill and its history store keeps it in the details tier;
the [GUI](../crates/gui.md) draws the inspector's ability list as the tree
([The Ability Tree In The Inspector](../decisions/ability-tree-in-the-inspector.md));
the [MCP](../crates/mcp.md) `breakdown` / `stored_fight` rows carry `group`,
`casts`, `avg_cast` and `parts`, with `ability_groups` beside them.

Step 2 stacks the drill's graph on the same entries: `SpellTree::entries`
(in the model, so the daemon ranking the stack and the window drawing the
tree share one definition of an entry), `Segment::ability_series` over
Damage's per-spell series and a new Healing one, and `Segment::target_series`
for an open ability over [R24](r24.md)'s per-unit series — built for the
window alone.

Step 4 nests a talent proc under the spell that drives it. The client links
no proc to its driver in data a generator can read — the proc hangs off the
talent's aura, whose class masks select nothing — so the table is curated in
the extractor and proven from both sides before it is emitted:
[gen-proc-spells](../tools/gen-proc-spells.md) requires the client's own text
(or a trigger effect) to tie each pair, and
[census-proc-spells](../tools/census-proc-spells.md) counts every pair in real
logs, pets folded onto their summoners. The new group kind is
[`wowdps-proto`](../crates/proto.md)'s `PROTO_VERSION` 37
([A Talent Proc Nests Under Its Driver](../decisions/proc-under-its-driver.md)).

Gated by [`tree.txt`](../fixtures/tree.md) — `check.awk`'s `casts`,
`damage_periodic` and `heal_periodic` on every fixture — and
`crates/core/tests/tree.rs` (structure, every fixture's trees, lazy = full, the
merge).

## Casts that began (v43)

Each row also counts its `SPELL_CAST_START`s (`SpellMeta::starts`), keyed and
gated exactly like its casts — so a cast-time spell whose starts outnumber its
casts was cancelled, kicked or moved out of that many times. The scanner never
counts the line, so the passive gate is what keeps it out of segmentation, and
[tree.txt](../fixtures/tree.md)'s nine starts (one cancelled, four that land
nowhere) gate it through `check.awk`'s `cast_starts` and the lazy-load
picture. `SPELL_EMPOWER_START` / `_END` parse as `Event::Empower` with END's
stage and nothing reads them yet. The field rides the wire under
[Armor Is Mitigation](../decisions/armor-is-mitigation.md)'s v43 bump.
