**The call:** A player's Damage and Healing by-ability rows NEST, at READ time: a pet's (or guardian's) rows hang under the spell that SUMMONED it — its first SPELL_SUMMON, by the player or a unit of theirs, keyed by the pet's NAME as the rows are — or under the pet itself when no SUMMON was seen or the summon is a hunter's "Call Pet N"; a spell only one trinket grants (the generated item table's owner, `class_spells` vetoing first as R12's marks do) hangs under the item, as do a trinket-summoned pet's rows; and every row splits into its `(spell id, periodic)` PARTS, which sum to it exactly. Each row also carries its CASTS: every `SPELL_CAST_SUCCESS` by the player and their pets under the row's name, through the passive gate. Step 3: its MISSES — every `*_MISSED` by the player and their pets under the row's name — and, on the player's own row, a DoT's UPTIME: the union of time their DEBUFF of that name is up on any enemy. Nothing opens, extends or splits a segment; lazy = full.

**A conflicting change would:** Let a pet's abilities scatter among the player's, lose a DoT's tick share inside one row, count a precast or a post-kill cast, or hang a class spell under a trinket.

## Detail

GROUPS: `Segment::spell_tree(player, view)` → `SpellTree { groups, rows }`, empty for every view but Damage and Healing. A pet row ("spell\0pet") takes its group from the segment's `summons` — `(raw summoner guid, pet name) → (id, name)`, first SUMMON wins, kept on the `Meter` (seeded into every segment like `owners`, written into the latest segment too; every SUMMON is an index seed, so a lazy load agrees) and read for the summoners that fold onto the player, the lowest summon id winning when two of theirs summoned one name: `Summon` (`"summon:{spell name}"`, the spell's icon) — unless the summon spell is a trinket's (`Item`, below) or named "Call Pet …" (a stable's button, not the pet: `Pet`, `"pet:{pet name}"`, the spell's icon); no SUMMON seen → `Pet` with no icon (0 = the reader draws its first member's). A player's own row takes `Item` (`"item:{item id}"`, the item's name, no icon) when its first-seen spell id is no class's and one trinket owns it (`item_spells::trinket_of`, generated: each trinket's effect spells chased two EffectTriggerSpell levels, a spell kept only when every trinket reaching it bears ONE ItemSparse name — the lowest id of it; a stat proc a hundred trinkets share belongs to none; a spell the kind table files as a consumable is never a trinket's). A group of one is still a group on the wire; readers draw it as its row with the group's word after it. • PARTS: `SpellSlot::parts` splits every record — all views, so the invariant holds everywhere — by `(id, periodic)`: a Damage event's `periodic` (`_PERIODIC_` in the event name), a Heal's (`SPELL_PERIODIC_HEAL`), false for an absorb credit and every count view. `SpellMeta.parts` is sent only for a row with two or more, sorted by `(spell id, periodic)`; Σ amount / extra / count / crits = the row's. `Segment::periodic_amount(player, view)` = Σ the periodic parts. • CASTS: counted per raw caster guid per spell NAME in the segment the passive gate admits (`open_segment_for_passive`: an open segment not past the trash gap) for any caster the segment controls (`Player-` / `Pet-`, or one it knows an owner of) — so a precast before ENCOUNTER_START, a cast after the kill, one in the trash dead zone and a hostile NPC's land nowhere — and folded at read like `actors`. A row's casts join by name (a pet's by its pet name); a cast with no row under it (a summon, a buff) is in `Segment::casts(player)` and on no row. A swing or a proc has none. • MISSES AND UPTIME (step 3): a `*_MISSED` line by a unit the segment controls, not its own target, counts once on the attacker's row by name (Miss % = misses / (hits + misses)); a player's `Debuff` on an enemy keeps a per-(player, debuff name) union over its targets — applied or refreshed adds one, removed drops one, time accruing while any holds it, one still open closing at read time — so a DoT on two enemies at once counts once; `Segment::misses_dealt` and `dot_uptime_ms` are the sums the fixture gates. • MERGE: an Overall's parts, casts and summons are its members' (summons first-seen in member order). • STORE (v36): the details tier keeps each player's `damage_tree` / `heal_tree` (`proto::history::spell_tree_json`; kinds by name, parts' `periodic` a bool); a details file written before v36 reads an empty tree and its abilities stand flat; the stored Damage / Healing drill answers with it. • Gated by `fixtures/tree.txt` (a Destruction Warlock: a Sayaad and an Infernal summoned mid-pull, Wither's hit and tick under two ids, Araz's Ritual Forge's two effects under one name, Eradicating Arcanocore; a Priest's Shadow Word: Pain hit and ticks under ONE id, Renew's instant heal and ticks, Flash Heal; a precast, a cast after the kill, a hostile cast, a cast before the trash's first hit and one in its dead zone) — `check.awk`'s `casts`, `damage_periodic` and `heal_periodic` on every fixture — and `crates/core/tests/tree.rs` (the groups, the parts, casts per row, the hunter's "Call Pet 1", every tree's shape on every fixture and Overall, lazy = full, the merge).

## Where it lives

The feed side is small and passive, in [`wowdps-core`](../crates/core.md)'s
`meter.rs`: every by-spell slot splits its tally into `(spell id, periodic)`
parts as it records (all views, so Σ parts = the row everywhere), casts count
through the same passive gate [R17](r17.md)'s misses and [R18](r18.md)'s
auras use, and a SPELL_SUMMON remembers its spell per (summoner, pet name) on
the `Meter` beside [R22](r22.md)'s `summoned` map. Casts never were combat, so
the index scanner has nothing new to mirror; summons are already index seeds,
so a lazy load agrees. The grouping happens at READ time in
`Segment::spell_tree`, over the same actor fold [R5](r5.md)'s pet rows use.

The trinket half leans on [R12](r12.md)'s generated item table, which now
also names each trinket spell's single owning item — [gen-item-spells](../tools/gen-item-spells.md)
reads ItemSparse for the names — with `class_spells` ([R8](r8.md)) vetoing
first, exactly as the R12 marks do.

The shape is [`wowdps-model`](../crates/model.md)'s `SpellTree`, a trailing
field of every `Breakdown` in [`wowdps-proto`](../crates/proto.md)
(`PROTO_VERSION` 36); [`wowdps-daemon`](../crates/daemon.md)'s engine fills it
on a Damage or Healing drill and its history store keeps it in the details tier;
the [GUI](../crates/gui.md) draws the inspector's ability list as the tree
([The Ability Tree In The Inspector](../decisions/ability-tree-in-the-inspector.md));
the [MCP](../crates/mcp.md) `breakdown` / `stored_fight` rows carry `group`,
`casts`, `avg_cast` and `parts`, with `ability_groups` beside them.

Step 2 stacks the drill's graph on the same entries: `SpellTree::entries`
(in the model, so the daemon ranking the stack and the window drawing the
tree share one definition of an entry), `Segment::ability_series` over
Damage's per-spell series and a new Healing one, and `Segment::target_series`
for an open ability over [R24](r24.md)'s per-unit series — built for the
window alone.

Gated by [`tree.txt`](../fixtures/tree.md) — `check.awk`'s `casts`,
`damage_periodic` and `heal_periodic` on every fixture — and
`crates/core/tests/tree.rs` (structure, every fixture's trees, lazy = full, the
merge).
