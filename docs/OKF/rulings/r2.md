**The call:** Amount = effective (amount − overheal); extra = overheal. SPELL_ABSORBED credits the ABSORBER; stagger/cheat-death self-absorbs excluded. (v44) A heal a heal-absorb ate is healing all the same: its eaten part (the line's `absorbed`, capped at amount − overheal) stays in the amount and is SURFACED as `heal_absorbed`, the third half of the healing split.

**A conflicting change would:** Move healing totals; double-count absorbs against R3; subtract a heal-absorb's part from healing done.

## Detail

Row amount = effective healing (amount − overheal); extra = overheal. SPELL_ABSORBED credits the ABSORBER with healing (no overheal component). The damage-event absorbed field never contributes to any healing number. Stagger / cheat-death self-absorbs (114556, 31850, 31230, 115069) excluded from healing. • AMENDMENT (the healing split and healing received; role pivots step 3a): per player, `overheal` = the Healing row's `extra`; `absorbed` = the absorber-credited R3 total, a dedicated counter written at the credit site AFTER the `NON_HEALING_ABSORBS` early return and into the segment `record` just chose (it may gap-split), so `absorbed ≤ healing` holds and it equals the fixture's awk `absorbheal`. `healed_received` = effective healing (amount − overheal) landing on a player FROM ANY SOURCE — NPC heals included, symmetric with R17 counting NPC attackers — with the `NON_HEALING_ABSORBS` family excluded exactly as above; ABSORBS ARE NOT RECEIVED HEALING (a consumed shield is damage prevented, already the R17 record's `absorbed`). `self_healed` = the subset with RAW `src.guid == dst.guid` — the line's guids before any fold, so a Hunter's Mend Pet is the owner's `received` (the pet folds), never their `self_healed`. A heal on a pet is its owner's received (raw-guid keyed, folded at read time, like R17's mitigation map). Held in a per-player `Healed { received, self_healed }` record beside the R17 record — never on `Mitigation`, whose wire codec would change. Identity, asserted on every fixture in the form the tests state it (`tests/support.rs`, `tests/real_log_support.rs`): over the fold keys G (every guid the slice mentions) and the friendly names F, Σg∈G Healing `by_target(g)` over labels in F = Σg∈G `healed(g).received` (ANY source) + Σg∈G `absorbed_healing(g)` − Σ SPELL_ABSORBED amount on non-friendly victims (parsed lines; an NPC's own shield credits its absorber but its victim is not in F). The left side is what the Healing drills say landed on friendlies — heals AND absorb credit, from every actor (an NPC healer has a drill even without a row); on `support.txt` 93 000 = 78 000 + 15 000 − 0, on `sample.txt`'s Verkath 56 400 = 47 000 + 12 800 − 3 400. Dropping the NPC term from both sides gives the player-sources form the fixture's expected file works through (88 000 = 73 000 + 15 000). A conflicting change would count a shield twice (once as prevented, once as received), or drop NPC heals and break the identity.


**Amendment (v44, the healing a heal-absorb ate).** A heal line logs its full
amount with the part a heal-absorb took — a boss's heal-absorb debuff, Light
of the Martyr's drawback, Death Pact — in its `absorbed` field (suffix offset
34). It stays healing done, as Warcraft Logs counts it; per healer per spell
name (raw source guid, folded at read like [R26](r26.md)'s casts)
`heal_absorbed` += `min(absorbed, amount − overheal)`. The cap matters: a
Shadow Priest's self-heals log more eaten than healed (56 of 6 201 eaten
lines in a real night's slice), and with it `heal_absorbed ≤ healing` holds
line by line. It rides `Row.heal_absorbed` on the Healing meter and
by-ability rows (0 elsewhere and on rows the per-second series build), the
card, the rows tier and SQL's `players.heal_absorbed`; `healed_received` is
unchanged; `SPELL_HEAL_ABSORBED` stays `Other`.
