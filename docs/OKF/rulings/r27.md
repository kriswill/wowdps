**The call:** Every `SPELL_ENERGIZE` / `SPELL_PERIODIC_ENERGIZE` whose amounts read (else `Other`) is `Event::Energize` — the suffix `amount, overEnergize, powerType, maxPower` — and the PLAYER it lands on (a `Player-` destination guid; a pet's pool is its own and counts for nobody) keeps per power type `gained` = Σ amount (what reached the pool), `wasted` = Σ overEnergize (generated while the pool was full) and the line count: `Segment::energize` → `Vec<EnergizeRow>`, ascending by type, merged by Σ into an Overall. Passive: never combat for the scanner, through the passive gate. Stored on the details tier per player and answered on `StoredFight.energize` for the drilled player (v43).

**A conflicting change would:** Fold a pet's focus or energy into its owner's pool of the same type, count a capped gain as gained, or open a segment from a resource tick and break scanner/meter lockstep.

## Detail

THE FORMAT (a real Heroic raid pull, 2026-09-27: 16 004 `SPELL_ENERGIZE` and 2 470 periodic lines, all 35 fields — the advanced block describing the destination, then the four-field suffix): the amounts are four-decimal numbers (`1.0000`, `0.5000` — fractions are real: half a mana tick), and a gain that hit the cap logs `amount 0.0000` with the whole generation as `overEnergize` (2 201 such lines: a Hunter's focus at 125 of 125 logging `0.0000,3.0000,2,125`), so `amount` is what reached the pool and `overEnergize` what the cap ate — the pool's generation is their sum and `EnergizeRow::waste_pct` the wasted share of it. A line whose amount or overcap does not read as a finite, non-negative number is `Other` (Rust's float parser also takes `inf` and `NaN`, which no log writes and which would poison every sum they reached). KEYED BY the destination's RAW guid, players only: a pet's energy or focus is a separate pool of the same type, so folding it onto its owner would mix two pools (the `tree.txt` Sayaad's 10 energy is nobody's). The source is not kept: the question is the player's capping, not who fed it. PASSIVE: the index scanner never counts the line, so it goes through `open_segment_for_passive` — one before a pull, after its end or past the trash gap lands nowhere (full = lazy) — and it is no R8, R23 or R11 signal. STORE: each details player writes `energize` (`power_type`, `gained`, `wasted`, `count`; absent before v43 = empty), and `StoredFight.energize` carries the drilled player's (empty without a drill, below the details tier). `check.awk` sums it independently over power types as `energize_gained` / `energize_wasted` (four decimals) on every fixture; `tree.txt` holds a warlock's shard fragments (a whole one, a capped one, a half), a priest's mana, a pet's energy, one before the pull and one in the dead zone. The power type's name is the reader's (`wowdps_model::power_name`: 0 mana, 1 rage, 2 focus, 3 energy, … 17 fury, 19 essence).

## Where it lives

The parser's `Event::Energize` and the meter's per-player ledger are
[`wowdps-core`](../crates/core.md)'s; the row is
[`wowdps-model`](../crates/model.md)'s `EnergizeRow` (with `power_name` for
readers); [`wowdps-proto`](../crates/proto.md) carries it on the details tier
and trailing `StoredFight`, which [`wowdps-daemon`](../crates/daemon.md)'s
store fills for the drilled player and [`wowdps-mcp`](../crates/mcp.md)'s
`stored_fight { player }` answers as `energize`. Gated by
[tree.txt](../fixtures/tree.md) and `check.awk`'s `energize_gained` /
`energize_wasted`; one line in [sample.txt](../fixtures/sample.md). The wire
field rides [Armor Is Mitigation](../decisions/armor-is-mitigation.md)'s v43
bump. A pet's pool is deliberately nobody's, unlike every other ledger here,
which folds pets onto owners ([R5](r5.md)): two pools of one type would read
as one.
