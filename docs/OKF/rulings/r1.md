**The call:** Row amount = event `amount + absorbed`; extra = overkill (≥0). SWING_DAMAGE counted, its LANDED twin → Other; `*_SUPPORT` is never damage (R19: the six share families are `Event::Support`, the rest Other); DAMAGE_SPLIT excluded.

**A conflicting change would:** Move every fixture damage total; break the "absorbed damage is damage done" convention shared with in-game meters.

## Detail

Row amount = per-event `amount + absorbed-field` (absorbed-by-shield damage counts as damage done, meter convention); extra = overkill clamped ≥ 0. Count SWING_DAMAGE only (SWING_DAMAGE_LANDED → Other); `*_SUPPORT` never contributes damage — the six R19 families parse as `Event::Support` (a share of a hit already counted here), every other `_SUPPORT` name → Other; DAMAGE_SPLIT excluded from offensive totals.
