**The call:** Row amount = event `amount + absorbed`; extra = overkill (≥0). SWING_DAMAGE counted, its LANDED twin → Other; `*_SUPPORT` is never damage (R19: the six share families are `Event::Support`, the rest Other); DAMAGE_SPLIT excluded. A hit the target's shield absorbed WHOLE — a `*_MISSED` line whose missType is ABSORB — is a hit too (2026-09-30): amount 0 + absorbed = its `amountMissed`, filed for any attacker, a player's or an NPC's, everywhere a partial absorb's absorbed part is (the attacker's Damage row, R24's enemy row, R17's Taken), never an R26 miss, and through the passive gate — the scanner ignores `*_MISSED`, so it never opens, extends or splits a segment.

**A conflicting change would:** Move every fixture damage total; break the "absorbed damage is damage done" convention shared with in-game meters.

## Detail

Row amount = per-event `amount + absorbed-field` (absorbed-by-shield damage counts as damage done, meter convention); extra = overkill clamped ≥ 0. Count SWING_DAMAGE only (SWING_DAMAGE_LANDED → Other); `*_SUPPORT` never contributes damage — the six R19 families parse as `Event::Support` (a share of a hit already counted here), every other `_SUPPORT` name → Other; DAMAGE_SPLIT excluded from offensive totals. • AMENDED BY R22: damage an actor deals to ITSELF (own pets folded) is not damage done — it never reaches a Damage row and is tallied apart as `self_harm`.

## Amended by

[R22 Self-harm](r22.md): damage an actor deals to itself (its own summons
folded in) is not damage done — it never reaches a Damage row and is tallied
apart as `self_harm`.

[A Hit A Shield Took Whole Is A Hit](../decisions/whole-absorb-is-a-hit.md)
(2026-09-30): the convention above, applied to the whole hit — a
`*_MISSED` ABSORB counts as the attacker's damage and the victim's Taken, so
an enemy's shield no longer eats a hit from the attacker's row while the game
still shares it ([R19](r19.md)). [R17](r17.md)'s `prevented` became the full
blocks alone, which keeps `mitigated_pct` the same number.
