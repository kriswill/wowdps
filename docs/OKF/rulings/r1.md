**The call:** Row amount = event `amount + absorbed − overkill` (2026-10-02: the log's amount includes the part past the victim's last health point, and the game's own meter counts none of it); extra = overkill (≥0), still reported. Every ledger the hit reaches takes the same amount — R17's Taken, R22's self-harm and friendly fire, R24's enemy row, R21's cells, R12/R26's series. SWING_DAMAGE counted, its LANDED twin → Other; `*_SUPPORT` is never damage (R19: the six share families are `Event::Support`, the rest Other); DAMAGE_SPLIT excluded. A hit the target's shield absorbed WHOLE — a `*_MISSED` line whose missType is ABSORB — is a hit too (2026-09-30): amount 0 + absorbed = its `amountMissed`, filed for any attacker, a player's or an NPC's, everywhere a partial absorb's absorbed part is (the attacker's Damage row, R24's enemy row, R17's Taken), never an R26 miss, and through the passive gate — the scanner ignores `*_MISSED`, so it never opens, extends or splits a segment.

**A conflicting change would:** Move every fixture damage total; break the "absorbed damage is damage done, overkill is not" convention the game's own meter keeps.

## Detail

Row amount = per-event `amount + absorbed-field − overkill` (absorbed-by-shield damage counts as damage done, meter convention; the overkill does not, 2026-10-02); extra = overkill clamped ≥ 0, so an overkill report still has it. Reconciled against the game's built-in meter on a real +15: four of a Warlock's abilities matched it to the thousand as `amount + absorbed + whole absorbs − overkill`, and a Windwalker's Touch of Death is half overkill. The death recap ([R9](r9.md)) keeps the raw hit and names its overkill apart. Count SWING_DAMAGE only (SWING_DAMAGE_LANDED → Other); `*_SUPPORT` never contributes damage — the six R19 families parse as `Event::Support` (a share of a hit already counted here), every other `_SUPPORT` name → Other; DAMAGE_SPLIT excluded from offensive totals. • AMENDED BY R22: damage an actor deals to ITSELF (own pets folded) is not damage done — it never reaches a Damage row and is tallied apart as `self_harm`.
