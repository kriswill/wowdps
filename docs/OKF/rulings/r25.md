**The call:** A meter snapshot carries the watched segment's RAID TIMELINE, computed at READ time: the view's raid series (`View::raid_series` — effective healing on Healing, damage taken on Taken and Deaths, damage on every other view) summed on the R12 1 s grid over every actor with a FRIENDLY meter row (owner a player, pets folded, an arena's enemy team left out), so Σ series = Σ the friendly rows' amounts of that view; every kept R9 death window in time order with its killing blow (the window's newest damage entry) and its R23 rez — an arena's hostile team's kept and flagged `enemy`; and the lust windows (the lust family's R18 spans on the group, unioned). Opens, extends or splits nothing; never stored — the history store REBUILDS a stored fight's from its tiers. R9 amendment: every recap entry carries `offset_ms`, its time before the death.

**A conflicting change would:** Sum pets twice or the arena's enemies into the raid curve, mark a death the Deaths rows do not count, or let the ribbon disagree with the meter's own total.

## Detail

THE SERIES: `Segment::raid_timeline(view)` reads `series` / `heal_series` / `taken_series` exactly as R12 and R17 keep them (raw actor guids, folded through `resolve_owner`) and keeps an actor iff its owner is a player and not an arena enemy (reaction bit 0x40 inside an arena) — the `rows` filter, so the identity is exact save R12's `MAX_BUCKETS` clamp; `bucket_ms` is 1000 and the client smooths (the window's ribbon draws 10 s rates, finer in a pull under two minutes). `view` names the series the timeline holds. • DEATHS: one `RaidDeath` per window the segment kept (`RECAP_DEATHS_CAP`; a dropped window is not marked, so `deaths.len()` + Σ `deaths_dropped` = Σ the Deaths rows), `index` the window's R9 index (what a Deaths drill asks for), `at_ms` rebased onto the segment's start, name / class / spec as the segment knows them. The killing blow is the window's newest non-gain entry — a `SPELL_INSTAKILL` included, at the health it took — its ability, its source's name (empty for a nil unit), its amount as `hit` and its overkill when above 0 (R1 clamps the log's −1 to 0, so 0 is "none"); a window with no damage entry leaves `blow` and `source` empty and `hit` 0. The rez is the R23 death span opened at the window's own millisecond, when a `SPELL_RESURRECT` closed it: `at_ms` its close, `by` the rezzer's raw guid — the player's own on a self-rez, which R23's mark leaves empty — and the spell's name; a span closed by an action alone is no rez, and neither is a `SPELL_RESURRECT` naming no spell (its R23 mark is a bare `Death`, which a stored fight could not tell from a span an action closed). Sorted by (at_ms, guid, index). `mine` is never the meter's: the daemon's owner resolution sets it (wire v35). `enemy` is the rows' R13 test on the dead player's own guid (inside an arena, reaction bit 0x40): the other team dies in the list — kept, so it still holds every window the Deaths rows count — and a reader counting the GROUP's deaths or battle rezzes skips it. `Rez.by_name` is the rezzer's name as the segment knows the guid (the guid itself when it knows none). A BATTLE REZ (`RaidDeath::battle_rezzed`) is a rez by someone else: a self-rez (Reincarnation, `by` = the dead player) undoes a death and is none. • LUST: `LUST_SPELLS` = Bloodlust 2825, Heroism 32182, Time Warp 80353, Ancient Hysteria 90355, Netherwinds 160452, Primal Rage 264667, Fury of the Aspects 390386, Harrier's Cry 466904, Voidlust 1277482 — each an `External` in the R18 table (a unit test holds the two together). Their spans on raid members (the capped list; one still open closes at read time against `close_ms`, as every R18 span does) are sorted by start and unioned — a span starting at or before the window's current end extends it — and each window wears its first span's name. An Overall's windows are its members' spans on the visit clock. • RECAP OFFSETS (R9 amendment): each recap Row's `offset_ms` = its entry's ts − the window's death ts, clamped at 0 and held non-increasing down the newest-first recap (a clock that stepped back mid-ring reads the newer entry's offset); attacker rows and every other row carry `None`. The rows tier writes it on recap events only. • STORED (v35): a stored fight's answer (`StoredFight.raid`) is rebuilt by the store from what its tiers keep, and equals the live timeline on everything the tiers hold: the deaths from the rows tier's recaps (R9 — killing blow = each window's newest damage row, its label split at its last " (" into ability and source only when the part in parentheses is one of that window's attackers and the whole label is not — the attackers are keyed by the source's name, or by the ability's for a nil source, so a nil-source ability whose own name ends in a parenthetical stays whole, as live has it (a recap kept without attackers splits as said); name, class, spec and team off the card), each rez from the dead player's R23 death mark of the same moment in their coarse mark list (label `Death (Spell)`; an empty caster is a self-rez), the lust windows from the friendly players' coarse `External` marks of the lust family unioned exactly as live (`meter::lust_windows`, shared), and the series from the details tier's 1 s damage or healing when it is on disk, else the coarse 10 s taken (Taken, Deaths) or healing — Σ equal to the live series — and none for damage once the details are demoted (the reader draws the axis, the lust and the deaths alone). A card-only answer (tier 1) carries `None`. A key's member boss parsed on demand answers the same rebuild over its fresh extract. `crates/daemon/tests/history.rs` holds stored = live on the sample, arena and spans fixtures, and on a synthetic pull with a battle rez, a self-rez and a death to a nil-source "Blight (Heroic)". • PARITY: everything is read-time over state that is already lazy = full, so a slice's replay, a full replay and a checkpoint-resumed scan answer the same timeline; `crates/core/tests/raid.rs` gates the Σ identity on every view, segment and Overall of every fixture, the death order and killing blows against the recaps, the offsets, the lust union and the rez on a synthetic pull, and lazy = full = checkpoint.

## Where it lives

The read side is `Segment::raid_timeline` in [`wowdps-core`](../crates/core.md)'s
`meter.rs`, over state other rulings already keep: the 1 s series of
[R12](r12.md) (damage, effective healing) and [R17](r17.md) (damage taken),
the death windows of [R9](r9.md), the death spans of [R23](r23.md) (which now
remember the resurrect's spell and rezzer) and the role spans of [R18](r18.md)
for the lust family. Nothing is written at feed time, so the index scanner and
lazy loading have nothing new to mirror.

The shape is [`wowdps-model`](../crates/model.md)'s `RaidTimeline`, carried as a
trailing field of every meter `Snapshot` by [`wowdps-proto`](../crates/proto.md)
(`PROTO_VERSION` 35, with `Row.mine` and the recap's `offset_ms` — see
[The Raid Timeline And Whose Rows On The Wire](../decisions/raid-timeline-and-mine-on-the-wire.md)).
[`wowdps-daemon`](../crates/daemon.md)'s engine attaches it to a live snapshot, its
history store rebuilds it for a stored fight (`StoredFight.raid`), and both mark the
account's deaths `mine`; the [GUI](../crates/gui.md) draws it as the ribbon
under the fight header and, on the Deaths view, as the chronological deaths
table; the [MCP](../crates/mcp.md) `fight` tool answers it as `raid`.

Gated by `crates/core/tests/raid.rs` over every committed fixture — the Σ
identity on every view, segment and Overall, the deaths against the recaps,
the offsets, lazy = full = checkpoint — with [`spans.txt`](../fixtures/spans.md)'s
Time Warp as the lust on a fixture and a synthetic pull for the union and the
rez.
