**The call:** One source per direction: SPELL_ABSORBED → healing, the damage-event absorbed field → damage.

**A conflicting change would:** Double-count absorbs in one view or drop them from the other.

## Detail

SPELL_ABSORBED is the sole source for absorb-as-healing; the damage-event absorbed field is the sole source for absorb-as-damage. Different views, different actors — no double count.
