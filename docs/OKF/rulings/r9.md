**The call:** First-death order; per-player 32-event ring (damage w/o absorbed part + gains), drained at UNIT_DIED into ONE WINDOW PER DEATH (v28, capped at 16 per player, oldest dropped and counted); SPELL_INSTAKILL is a recap entry for the health it took; recap newest-first with hp/gain; never opens a segment.

**A conflicting change would:** Break recap parity between lazy and full replay, or reorder the Deaths view.

## Detail

`rows(Deaths)` lists players in FIRST-death order, amount = 1 per death. Each Segment keeps a bounded per-player ring (`RECAP_CAP = 32`) of recent events on that player: damage hits (amount = the per-event `amount` alone — the absorbed part never touched their health and appears as its own gain entry) and gains (heals at effective value with overheal in extra; consumed absorbs via SPELL_ABSORBED). UNIT_DIED drains the ring into a NEW window for that death, appended oldest-first (v28: a player who died three times keeps three; earlier windows used to be overwritten, so a tank's first two deaths were unrecoverable). Windows are capped per player at `RECAP_DEATHS_CAP = 16`, dropping the OLDEST and counting them (`deaths_dropped`), so `windows + dropped` always reconciles with the Deaths row's count — which stays the authoritative tally, incremented once per UNIT_DIED and independent of the ring. A visit's Σ APPENDS its members' windows in member order rather than replacing them. `breakdown_at(guid, Deaths, Some(i))` picks one; `breakdown` (and every pre-v28 caller) is the LAST death. So `breakdown(guid, Deaths)` returns (timeline newest-first, killing blow with its overkill-in-extra leading; attacker totals sorted desc, gains excluded, source-less damage bucketed under its spell name). Timeline rows carry `hp`/`gain`; labels "{spell} ({source})", spell alone when the source unit is nil. HP comes from the line's own advanced block when it describes the victim, else back-fills from the next advanced line describing them within 1s onto the OLDEST DAMAGE entry still missing it, and only when no such damage entry exists onto the OLDEST gain entry still missing it (v28 fix: several hits landing in one instant each get their own report, paired FIFO the way the client emits swing + LANDED twin; filling the NEWEST empty entry instead misfiled the first report onto the last hit and DISCARDED the rest, so a player killed by simultaneous melees showed a healthy mid-sequence floor and no zero at all; v28 amendment: damage before gains, because a shield consumed and a swing landing in one instant both arrive empty — SPELL_ABSORBED precedes the hit it softened — and a plain oldest-first pick handed the one report to the absorb, leaving the hit, the entry a recap exists to explain, with no health forever; a gain entry that stays empty is the lesser loss) (SWING_DAMAGE → its LANDED twin; SPELL_ABSORBED → the paired damage line). SCRIPTED KILLS: a mechanic (or a cheat-death effect expiring — Purgatory logs here, self-cast) ends a unit with SPELL_INSTAKILL, 13 fields, no advanced block and NO amount, and NO damage event accompanies it. Without an arm for it a death by mechanic leaves a recap of the last healthy seconds and no cause at all, which is the normal case on some encounters. It becomes a recap entry on a player destination: amount = the health it took (the victim's newest reported `current`, 0 if they never reported), `extra` = 0 (a stated hit above the remainder would be overkill, the R1 convention; the line carries no such number today), `hp` = (0, their max) — it ends them. RECAP ONLY: it is never a Damage or Taken amount, because no dealt-damage event balances it and R17's identity (Σ dealt to friendlies = Σ Taken + stagger) would break. Like every recap push it must never open or extend a segment — the scanner does not know the event at all. Health reports and recap bookkeeping never open or extend a segment; the ring is segment-local (lazy-load parity).

## Amended by R25

[R25](r25.md) (PROTO_VERSION 35) amends this ruling in its own row: every
recap entry carries `offset_ms`, its time before the death — ≤ 0 and held
non-increasing down the newest-first recap, so a clock that stepped back
mid-ring reads the newer entry's offset — and every other row carries
`None`. The rows tier of the history store writes it on recap events alone,
and a recap stored before v35 reads back without it. The same kept windows
are R25's deaths: one `RaidDeath` per window, its `index` this ruling's
window index, its killing blow the window's newest damage entry, so the
raid timeline never marks a death the Deaths rows do not count.

Where it is read: the window's inspector draws a recap's time column from
the offsets and its Deaths view lists the windows in the order they happened
([`wowdps-gui`](../crates/gui.md)); the mcp answers them as `offset_secs`
([`wowdps-mcp`](../crates/mcp.md)); `crates/core/tests/raid.rs` gates them
(`recap_offsets_are_before_the_death_and_never_rise`) — see
[The Raid Timeline And Whose Rows On The Wire](../decisions/raid-timeline-and-mine-on-the-wire.md).

## Feign Death is no death

Since 2026-10-03 a `UNIT_DIED` whose trailing `unconsciousOnDeath` field is
1 counts nothing: the parser reads it as `Event::Other` and the
[index](../crates/core.md) scanner's `is_combat` drops it alike, so the line
counts no death, freezes no recap window, opens no [R23](r23.md) span, and
opens or extends no segment (lazy = full). On players the flag is a Hunter's
Feign Death: 104 player lines over eight real logs, every one a Hunter's,
and the [R23 census](../decisions/a-death-ends-at-first-sight.md) had found
every "death" ending under half a second to be one. Creatures write it too
(a boss that goes down without dying), and the rule drops theirs as well,
though only their names were ever learned from a death. The
[sample fixture](../fixtures/sample.md) holds one for its hunter at
20:08:29.5 as a negative control — his deaths stay 1, and `check.awk` skips
the line the same way.

## The shields beside the health (v43)

The advanced block's field 9 is the unit's shields in total — the field
[FORMAT-NOTES](../../../crates/core/fixtures/FORMAT-NOTES.md) once called
versatility's slot (7) and two zeros. Since 2026-10-08 the parser's health
hint carries it (and the unit's power), and a recap entry takes it from the
same report as its health, so `Row::absorb` sits beside `hp` on every event
that has one: a killing blow on a player with a big shield left reads
differently from one with none. It rides the wire under
[Armor Is Mitigation](../decisions/armor-is-mitigation.md)'s v43 bump and
the history store's death windows; [`wowdps-core`](../crates/core.md)'s
`note_hp` back-fills it with the health. A scripted kill states no report,
so it has none.
