**The call:** Encounters = START..END; Trash = first..last combat event. RATES run on the COMBAT clock (2026-10-02, `Segment::combat_ms`): an encounter's START..END, a trash segment's ENGAGEMENT clock — the stretches between consecutive damage lines between the group and an enemy summed, a quiet stretch over 30 s (`ENGAGE_QUIET_MS`) left out — R7's first..last when it saw no engagement line at all.

**A conflicting change would:** Deflate trash DPS with idle time, unclock encounters, or rate trash over the walk between packs.

## Detail

Encounter segments = ENCOUNTER_START..ENCOUNTER_END exactly. Trash segments = FIRST..LAST combat event inside the segment (active combat time, like in-game meters) — never open..close, which counts idle time and deflates DPS.

The combat clock (amendment, 2026-10-02): `duration_ms` stays a segment's length; every rate divides by `combat_ms`. An engagement line is a DAMAGE line between a friendly, player-controlled unit (`0x10 | 0x100`) and a hostile or neutral one (`0x40 | 0x20`), either way round — never a passive `*_MISSED` line (it may not extend a segment, so the clock never outruns the duration), never friendly fire ([R22](r22.md)). Heals and HoT ticks still keep a segment open ([R4](r4.md)) but never run the clock — they are why a first cut over the meter's whole combat-line set held the clock open on the walk between packs. Calibrated on one real +15 (game 1600.4 s, this clock 1610.7 s, the key timer 1716.9 s); per segment, meter-only, lazy = full by construction.
