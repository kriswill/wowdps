**The call:** A player's UNIT_DIED opens a DEATH SPAN on them, closed by the resurrection that raised them (`SPELL_RESURRECT` — a battle rez, a Soulstone, an Ankh, a Mass Resurrection) and failing that by their first SIGHT ALIVE: a line whose advanced block describes them above zero health (a heal or a hit landing on them, their own cast or swing), or a cast of theirs. Damage they deal is no proof (their pets implode and their projectiles land after they die), nor is healing done. A span still open at the end reads `close_ms − at`, like an open R18 role span, and says so (v41 `Mark::open`); an Overall carries a member's open death into the next member, which ends it at its first sight of them. It rides every timeline flavor as a `MarkKind::Death` mark, so a flat stretch of damage, healing or damage taken finally says WHY it is flat.

**A conflicting change would:** Leave a reader guessing whether a zero curve is a dead player or a lazy one; or call a player alive because something they cast before dying landed after it.

## Where it lives

The span is [`wowdps-core`](../crates/core.md)'s: `Segment::death_spans` in
`meter.rs`, a map of its own so [R18](r18.md)'s `spans`, `uptime` and
`am_uptime` never see a death, closed by the parser's passive
`Event::Resurrect` (`SPELL_RESURRECT`, wire v30) or by `Segment::seen_alive` —
the advanced block's health report (`hp_hint`) above zero, or a cast —
through the passive gate, so neither opens nor extends a segment and the
index scanner has nothing to mirror. Each segment also keeps its first sight
of every player (`first_alive`), which an Overall uses to end a death a
member left open (a wipe) at the next member's first sight of them, and the
mark says whether it never closed (`open`, wire v41)
([A Death Ends Where They Are First Seen Alive](../decisions/a-death-ends-at-first-sight.md)). It reaches every timeline as a
`MarkKind::Death` mark (code 8) with the rez spell in its label and the
rezzer as its caster, and the history store writes it into the coarse mark
lists, where a stored fight reads it back.

Since [R25](r25.md) (PROTO_VERSION 35) a span also REMEMBERS the resurrect
that ended it — its spell and its rezzer's raw guid, the player's own on a
self-rez, which the mark leaves empty — so the raid timeline names each
death's rez and tells a battle rez from a Reincarnation; a
`SPELL_RESURRECT` naming no spell is no rez, since a stored fight could not
tell it from a span an action closed
([The Raid Timeline And Whose Rows On The Wire](../decisions/raid-timeline-and-mine-on-the-wire.md)).
The [GUI](../crates/gui.md)'s inspector draws a death on its graph as a red
rule where they died and a green one where they were alive again (none while
`open`), and its ribbon marks it with a skull; the overlay's graph
keeps it as a mark.

Gated by `meter.rs`'s `r23_*` unit tests (a death spans until the rez that
ended it; a rez alone opens no segment), `crates/core/tests/raid.rs`'s
synthetic pull for the rez the timeline names, and the ignored real-log
census `crates/core/tests/real_log_deaths.rs` — how real spans ENDED, since
a dead player's DoTs keep ticking after UNIT_DIED (on the 2026-10-02 log:
five deaths under a second before the sight-alive rule, none after) — plus
`r23_a_sight_of_them_alive_closes_a_death_and_an_open_one_runs_to_the_end`
and `r23_a_wipe_carries_its_deaths_into_the_next_pull_of_the_overall`.
