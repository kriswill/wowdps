**The call:** The six `*_SUPPORT` damage/heal families become `Event::Support` (pop the trailing supporter guid, dispatch on the base family with the spell prefix; amount = the buff's share); per player `given` / `received` / `targets`, raw-keyed and folded at read; passive gate only; `effective = damage − received + given` DERIVED, never stored, Σ effective = Σ damage; `Spec::support` is a flag only; `SPELL_ABSORBED_SUPPORT` → Other.

**A conflicting change would:** Double-count the Evoker's own procs (logged as both a hit and a support line), read a melee support line with the swing offsets, open a segment from a share and break scanner/meter lockstep, or grade Augmentation on a formula peers do not share.

## Detail

• FAMILIES: `SPELL_DAMAGE_SUPPORT`, `SPELL_PERIODIC_DAMAGE_SUPPORT`, `RANGE_DAMAGE_SUPPORT`, `SWING_DAMAGE_LANDED_SUPPORT` (the melee one — there is no `SWING_DAMAGE_SUPPORT`), `SPELL_HEAL_SUPPORT`, `SPELL_PERIODIC_HEAL_SUPPORT` (`parser::is_support_event`). Each is the base family's line with a 3-field spell block that is the BUFF (Ebon Might 395152, Prescience 410089, Shifting Sands 413984, Bombardments 434481, Fate Mirror 413786) and the supporter's bare guid as the LAST field in place of the `ST`/`AOE` trailer — so every support line is SPELL-shaped (a melee support line has 42 fields, not the swing's 38; read at a fixed swing offset (off28, awk $29) its amount would be the advanced block's `ui_map_id` — 2287 in the fixture — and THIS parser's swing path, probing f[9] for the advanced block and finding the buff's spell id instead of a guid, would read the amount as that spell id, 395152). The parser pops the trailing supporter (it must be a non-nil guid, else `Other`) and dispatches the remainder on the base family with the spell-block prefix, so the shared damage / heal suffix code reads the amount: `amount + absorbed` for damage (R1's convention, so `effective` adds and subtracts under the rule `damage` was counted by), or `amount − overheal` for a heal. The amount is the buff's SHARE of the hit (additive across buffs, far under the hit); a proc the Evoker owns outright (Bombardments, Fate Mirror) carries the whole hit — and the Evoker's own procs are logged TWICE, as `SPELL_DAMAGE src = Evoker` and as a support line naming the Evoker as supporter, same amount. Any other `_SUPPORT` name stays a duplicate → `Other`: `SPELL_ABSORBED_SUPPORT`'s spell block is the buff, the underlying shield is unknowable, so the `NON_HEALING_ABSORBS` exclusion cannot be applied. • METER: per segment, per player, raw-guid keyed and folded onto owners at read time (a buffed pet's support is its owner's received — support `src` is often a pet or guardian): `given.damage / given.healing` where `supporter` = the player, `received.*` where `src` = the player, and per supporter `targets` (per buffed player — keyed by the RAW buffed-source guid and walked to its owner at read, so a buffed pet's shares name the OWNER: damage, healing). A support line goes through the passive gate (`open_segment_for_passive`), never `ensure_combat`, never `last_ms`, never an R8 signal, never a mark; the scanner ignores every support family (`is_combat` uses exact lists). A share logged before the pull's first hit attributes to nowhere, full = lazy. R1 / R2 / R3 do not move: the hit's own line is the only damage or healing counted. • ONE NUMBER FOR EVERYONE: `effective = damage − received.damage + given.damage` (`model::effective`, exact in i128, clamped at 0). For a peer it is their net; for an Augmentation with no received it is its contribution; a self-supported proc (given and received by the same player) cancels, so it is counted once, by R1; and Σ effective over a segment = Σ damage — a true partition of the raid's damage. ACCEPTED DISTORTION: a guardian outside the log's filter (Army of the Dead ghouls) has its swings logged only as `SWING_DAMAGE_LANDED` with a `_LANDED_SUPPORT` share, so R1 counts no hit for it while the share is still received by its owner and given by the Evoker — the share moves from the owner to the Evoker's `given` with no R1 hit to net it against; the partition holds while the owner's own damage covers it, and `model::effective` clamps at 0 (never wraps) when it does not. Real logs show the shape (the real-log gate counts the clamp cases); R1 does not move for it. There is no `contribution` and no `net` field: `effective` is derived by readers from `damage` and the two scalars (the 2b rule: derived, never travels). `Spec::support()` (Augmentation only) is a flag for the fight card and the trend default; grading needs no support branch.
