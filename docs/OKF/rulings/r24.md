**The call:** Every damage event whose destination is an ENEMY — a `Creature-`/`Vehicle-` guid that is not ours (a guardian we summoned is a `Creature-` too; its own Stagger tick is R22's and never an enemy row) — and whose source IS ours (a `Player-`/`Pet-` guid, or a unit whose owner is a player as the segment knows it AT THE HIT: a `Creature-` guardian counts once its SPELL_SUMMON has been seen, a `Pet-` always does — even one orphaned by an R6 seam, because the enemy still took the bite) is recorded a third time on the DESTINATION as `View::EnemyTaken`: amount = R17's `amount + absorbed`, extra = absorbed, by-spell = the ability, by-target = the attacker's OWNER name (a pet's hits sit under its master in the drill — folded at RECORD time, unlike R17's attacker name, because the row is about who hurt the enemy). Rows are ONE PER ENEMY NAME — every hostile unit called "Gore Rattle" folds into one row, the way the game's own enemy meters read — keyed by that name (a drill asks by name and merges every hostile guid wearing it), listing on count > 0, with no class, no spec, no `hp` and never `enemy` (the R13 team split is a player-list affair). What an enemy dealt, and what one enemy did to another, is not on it. THE IDENTITY (`tests/taken.rs`): per segment, Σ over every friendly unit's Damage `by_target` for hostile NAMES — each unit once: an owned pet through its owner, an orphaned one through itself — = Σ EnemyTaken row amounts, and each row's two panes total the row. Segment-local; never opens, extends or splits a segment (it rides the Damage record's `ensure_combat`, so scanner/meter lockstep is untouched); an Overall's rows are its members' folded by name. A LIVE view only: `proto::history::VIEW_KEYS` deliberately omits it, so no card, rows document or DuckDB view carries enemy rows (a stored fight answers "no rows stored for this view") — a card is about the group.

**A conflicting change would:** Count our own guardian as an enemy, drop an orphaned pet's damage from the enemy's row, or split one boss across a dozen rows of the same name.


## Where it lives

The write side is one more block beside [R17](r17.md)'s in
[`wowdps-core`](../crates/core.md)'s `Meter::feed` Damage arm, gated by
`Segment::friendly_attacker` on BOTH ends of the event — the destination must
not be ours, the source must be. The read side is `Segment::enemy_rows`
(name-folded) and the `enemy` branch of `breakdown_at`, which merges every
hostile guid wearing the row's name. It reuses R17's amount convention and
R1's `ensure_combat`, so it adds nothing the index scanner has to know.

The view reaches every frontend through [`wowdps-model`](../crates/model.md)'s
`View::EnemyTaken` (index 7) and [`wowdps-proto`](../crates/proto.md)'s code 7
(`PROTO_VERSION` 32 — see [Enemy Taken Is A Live View](../decisions/enemy-taken-is-a-live-view.md)),
Shift-E in the [TUI](../crates/tui.md) and [GUI](../crates/gui.md), `enemy_taken`
in the [MCP tools](../crates/mcp.md), and the overlay's click-cycle.

Gated by [`taken.txt`](../fixtures/taken.md) and every other fixture through
`tests/taken.rs`'s `dealt_to_hostiles_equals_enemy_taken_on_every_segment`
(the identity, each friendly unit counted once) and the extended lazy-load
picture; the unit test `r24_enemy_taken_folds_by_name_and_lists_only_what_the_group_dealt`
pins the fold, the pet-under-master drill and the exclusions.

## Why it exists

The game's own enemy meters answer "how much has each boss and add taken from
us", one row per enemy name with the rate beside it. Everything that view
needs was already in the log — R17 records every hit on its DESTINATION —
but only for friendly destinations. Two edges shaped the ruling: `relog.txt`'s
version seam orphans a pet, so the identity counts every friendly UNIT once
rather than reading the player list; and `taken.txt`'s Niuzao is a
`Creature-` guid on OUR side, whose self-tick landed on the first cut as an
enemy row — hence "not ours" on the destination, not merely "hostile-prefixed".
