**The call:** Every Taken hit (R17's amount, the same lines; a miss is not a hit) on a friendly is bucketed, per HOSTILE DEBUFF open on the victim at that moment, by the debuff's LEVEL: a `Debuff` aura on a player or pet whose source the group does not control (a hostile NPC or the nil environment unit — a player's own debuff on a player never conditions; no table, no class veto) drives a per-(raw victim, spell) level ledger — `AuraApplied` = 1, `AuraDose` = its trailer (the new running total, both families), `AuraRefresh` unchanged, `AuraRemoved` gone; a dose / refresh / removal with no entry OPENS one at its level (the debuff predated the segment). Cells are `(victim, damage label + id, aura, level ≥ 1) → {hits, sum, max}`, one per open debuff (a hit under two lands in two), per-victim cap `STACK_CELL_CAP = 512` distinct cells newest-dropped with `dropped` counted; `stacking_debuffs` lists every debuff seen open (label, applier NAME the R17 by-target way, max level, Σ its cells' hits). Level 0 is DERIVED by the reader from the unconditioned by-ability row — hits and sum exactly, `max` UNKNOWN (never a maximum-of-all), and the row's count is R17's EVENTS so the derived count includes misses at any level. Every aura arm through the passive gate; the ledger is segment-local and never merges (an Overall's cells are Σ members' — hits and sum summed, max maxed, `seen` by max level and Σ hits); nothing on the timeline, the card, R17's totals or the scanner; lazy = full. STORE (6): the rows tier keeps `stacks[]` per friendly player with any cell or debuff — the debuffs seen and the RAW per-level cells, never the derived level 0 — so SQL derives it the daemon's way; empty on a pre-6 rows file, `regrade` fills it. WIRE (v27): `Breakdown` + `stacking` / `stacks` / `stacks_dropped`, Taken only.

**A conflicting change would:** Hide a one-shot at 3 stacks under a whole-pull mean, report a level-0 max the meter never measured, let a player's own Slow condition, or open a segment from a dose.

## Detail

THE FORMAT (census, a live Ruby Life Pools session, 2026-09-05): every `SPELL_AURA_APPLIED_DOSE` / `SPELL_AURA_REMOVED_DOSE` line is 14 fields — the 13 aura fields plus a trailing integer — and the trailer is the aura's NEW RUNNING TOTAL on both families (Hellbent Commander `REMOVED_DOSE …,3` → `,2` → `,1`; Tectonic Strike `APPLIED_DOSE …,2` then `,3`); no dose carries an absorb trailer; within one timestamp the application precedes its damage, so a stacking hit lands at its NEW level. 1698 of 1899 applied doses landed on players, 92 were debuffs, 11 debuffs on players — the conditioning population is tiny per pull and the buff side is the bulk the ruling ignores. • PARSER: `Event::AuraDose { src, dst, spell, aura_type, stacks: u16, removed }` reads the aura type at index 12 and the stacks at 13 as an integer, `Other` when absent or non-numeric — never gated on width (a 15-field line still reads its stacks at 13). Buff doses ARE emitted: a parser filter would make the fixture's negative controls untestable; the meter's filter decides. • ADMISSION is a data property, not a table: any `Debuff` on a friendly (`Player-` / `Pet-`, R17's destination rule) from a source `!controlled` (not a player, not an owned pet — the nil guid passes, so an environment puddle conditions, named `Environment`). Stacking tank-vulnerability debuffs are new every patch; a curated list could not keep up, and R18/R20's tables are not consulted. • THE LEDGER lives on the segment (`debuffs: (raw victim, spell) → {level, label, src}`), keyed RAW and folded onto owners at read time like `mitigation`: a debuff on a pet conditions the pet's hits, which fold onto the owner (fixture case 7). It is never merged: an Overall receives no events, its cells are the members' summed. • CELLS: in R17's Taken arm, after the hit is recorded, for each open entry on the raw destination `cells[(damage label, damage id, aura, level)] += {1, amount, max}` with R17's `amount + absorbed` (the shield-eaten part still landed); the label follows R17's by-ability key (`Melee` for a swing, the envType for environmental damage). A hit at the same millisecond as the dose that raised the level lands at the NEW level (log order). THE DEATH RULE: a removal CLOSES the entry at its millisecond rather than dropping it — the client strips a dying player's auras and writes those REMOVED lines BEFORE the killing blow at the same millisecond (retest 20: Tectonic Strike at 3 stacks, eight REMOVED lines, then the 622 644 blow, then UNIT_DIED one ms later), so a hit at the removal's millisecond still lands at the level and a hit any later does not; a dose / refresh / apply on a closed entry reopens it. Without this every killing blow under a stacked debuff files at the derived level 0 — the one hit a coach most wants conditioned. `seen[aura] = (label, applier name, max level, hits while open at any level)`. The per-victim cap counts distinct cells; a hit that would open a cell past it is `dropped += 1` and its `seen` hit still counts. • READ: `stacking_debuffs(guid)` — every debuff seen open (highest level desc, hits desc, id), so the reader lists `max_level ≥ 2` as stacking and can still condition on a level-1-only one by id; `stack_cells(guid)` — merged across the owner's raw guids, sorted by aura, damage id, label, level; `stacks_dropped(guid)`. LEVEL 0 (retest 21): the ledger also keeps an UNCONDITIONED BASELINE per victim per (damage label, damage spell ID) — `{hits, sum, misses}`, every Taken hit and every miss line, debuff or not, uncapped — because the Taken by-ability row is per NAME and two abilities can share one (Crushing Smash 372730 and 1305213 on one pack); `stack_base(guid)` folds it. Level 0 per id = baseline − Σ the aura's level ≥ 1 cells of that id: landed hits and sum EXACT, `misses` beside them (a miss is never conditioned, it lands in no cell), `max` unknown. Identity, fixture-gated: Σ over ids of (baseline hits + misses) = the name-merged row's count and Σ sums = its amount; every (aura, id) group fits inside its id's baseline. Amounts everywhere are R17's `amount + absorbed` (the 622 644 landed blow reads 623 907 with its 1 263 absorbed). • GATED by `fixtures/stacks.txt` (ten cases: the ladder 0 → 1 → 2 → 3 → refresh → down to 2 → 0 with the max at 3; Tectonic's own hit at its new level; two debuffs open at once; an orphan dose at 4 on the healer; a player-sourced Slow with a dose and a Hellbent Commander BUFF with doses up and down as negative controls; a pet's hit folding; a dodge at 3 stacks recording nothing; an absorbed hit at 2 counting `amount + absorbed`; an aura after the kill and a dose in the trash dead zone landing nowhere) with `check.awk`'s own level machine emitting `stack_hits / stack_sum / stack_max / stack_cells / stack_auras` per player, the per-cell table in `tests/stacks.rs`, and the ignored `real_log_stacks.rs` gate (every `_DOSE` line parses; Σ a cell group's hits / sum ≤ the Taken row on every boss pull — 32 952 dose lines, 449 groups on the census log).
