**The call:** Damage an actor dealt to ITSELF (own pets folded: source and destination resolving to the same owner) is NOT damage done — it never reaches a Damage row, drill, by-target, timeline or DPS. It is tallied apart per actor as `self_harm` — `total` and the `on_friendly` subset whose destination guid was `Player-`/`Pet-` — raw-source-keyed and folded at read like R17's mitigation map. Segmentation is untouched: these lines still open and extend segments exactly as before.

**A conflicting change would:** Put a Brewmaster's Stagger ticks (18% of his "damage" on a real key) back on the meter, or desync the scanner from the meter.

## Detail

A damage event whose folded source equals its folded destination (`Meter::fold_owner`, the write-side twin of `Segment::resolve_owner` — the fold has to happen BEFORE the amount is filed, and an advanced-format line carries its own owner hint, so a pet naming its owner on that very line already folds) is recorded ONLY as `Segment::self_harm(guid)` += `amount + absorbed` (and into `self_harm_on_friendly` when the DESTINATION guid is `Player-`/`Pet-`) — R1's amount convention, no overkill, no `petdamage`, no `bucket`/`spell_bucket` series entry. Every in-game meter reports what you did to the ENEMY; before this a Brewmaster's Stagger self-ticks (16.4M) and his Niuzao's own ticks (2.3M) were 21% of his Damage row on a real +14. R17 is unchanged — a stagger tick is still excluded from Taken and still counted as `stagger_ticked`, and non-stagger self-harm (Shadow Word: Death and friends) still lands on the victim's Taken row with themselves as the attacker, which is where a reader looks for it. THE IDENTITY, restated (`tests/taken.rs`, `tests/real_log_taken.rs`): per segment, Σ over every actor's Damage `by_target` for friendly NAMES + Σ `self_harm_on_friendly` = Σ Taken rows + Σ `stagger_ticked`. The `on_friendly` half, not the total, because R17's destination universe is a friendly GUID: a guardian summoned as a `Creature-` unit (Niuzao) folds onto its owner for every OFFENSIVE number but never earns a Taken row, so its self-harm sits in NEITHER side — the real +14 that motivated the ruling had 155 401 of exactly that on one pull, and the first cut of the identity failed on it. `check.awk`'s `friendlyGuid()` mirrors the same universe in the checker's `taken()` and stagger-tick arms. `ensure_combat` still runs on these lines, deliberately: the index scanner counts damage lines structurally without reading amounts, so skipping one here would break scanner/meter lockstep and lazy/full parity. Merges like every other raw-keyed ledger (`absorb`, so an Overall's `self_harm` is Σ its members'). No wire and no store change: `self_harm` is not on `Row` or `Mitigation`, so `PROTO_VERSION` stands — what a reader sees is a Damage row that finally matches the game's own meters. Fixtures: `taken.txt` (Zenlí's two self-ticks, 10 000 — Taken's `stagger_ticked` — plus a `Creature-` guid Niuzao summoned mid-pull whose Stomp still counts as 6 000 of DAMAGE and whose own 2 500 tick is `self_harm` alone, pinning `total` 12 500 apart from `on_friendly` 10 000) and `shields.txt` (Brewmoon's 1 500); `check.awk` computes the split independently and emits `self_harm` beside `damage`.

## Where it lives

The write-side split is one branch in [`wowdps-core`](../crates/core.md)'s
`Meter::feed` Damage arm; the read side is `Segment::self_harm` /
`self_harm_on_friendly`, folded onto owners exactly like [R17](r17.md)'s
mitigation map. It amends [R1](r1.md) (damage done) and leans on
[R4](r4.md)'s pet fold for the definition of "itself".

Gated by [`taken.txt`](../fixtures/taken.md) — where a `Creature-` guid Niuzao
is summoned mid-pull to hold `total` apart from `on_friendly` — and
[`shields.txt`](../fixtures/shields.md).

## Why it exists

A real +14 Voidscar Arena run put a Brewmaster fourth on the meter at 89.4M
while the group's in-game meters showed 66.7M. 16.4M was his own Stagger
ticks and 2.3M was his Niuzao's — 21% of a tank's "damage" was damage to
himself. The engine had always been right about the events; the Damage VIEW
was answering a different question from every meter the group could compare
it to. See [Keep Self-Harm Off The Damage Meter](../decisions/self-harm-off-the-damage-meter.md).
