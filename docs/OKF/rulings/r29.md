**The call:** Every boss pull (an Encounter segment; never an arena) and every keystone run (a keyed visit's Σ) is CUT from its parsed lines into the history store's REPLAY tier (`core::replay::cut` → `model::replay::Cut` → `proto::replay`, `replay/<fight id>.bin`): its units numbered in order of first sight; every advanced block that places a player, creature or vehicle on the FLOOR — the UiMap id the players were posted on most, a tie to the lower id — as a POST (x, y, facing, health share, power); the EVENT rows (a player's death, an NPC's death or fall unconscious, a rez; a hostile unit's cast begun or landed and a player's interrupt of it; a player's cast begun, landed or failed; a hostile spell's hit on a player where it found them, or its miss; an aura from a hostile unit or from no one going on a player, coming off, changing stacks); what players PLACED (the encounter rubric's generated table); the WORLD MARKERS standing on the instance map at the start and each change. Passive: it moves no meter number and opens no segment.

**A conflicting change would:** Number units by anything but first sight (every stored file's ids would shift), keep a post off the floor or a position the block did not give, store the raid's damage per second a second time (R25 answers it), or let a cut's line open a segment or seed the meter and break the scanner's lockstep.

## Detail

ONE READING: every line through `parser::parse_line` once (`cut_and_meter` feeds the same parse to the meter on an import), the SEEDS for state — the latest COMBATANT_INFO spec per player, the zone, the keystone, the markers standing per map (a marker number is one object: a placement moves it to its map, off any other; a removal takes it off every map) — and the SLICE for the rest, its first line t 0 (ENCOUNTER_START, or a run's first line) and then read as every other line. The index's seeds carry every line this reads from before a slice (COMBATANT_INFO, ZONE_CHANGE, CHALLENGE_MODE, and since v45 every WORLD_MARKER line — index cache magic `\x13`), so a lazy cut equals the cut over every line before it (`tests/replay.rs`). UNITS: numbered where first seen, line by line, the line's post before its event row (an event numbers its unit, then its source, then its target), the units only a placed row names after all others. A `Player-` unit is `player`, class and spec from COMBATANT_INFO, else R8 over the slice (the meter's evidence: damage, healing, an absorb's shield, an interrupt, an aura applied, a dispel; the first class wins, a spec only with it), named by the log, `you` = the owner's GUID (the card's owner when stored); a unit some modeled line flagged hostile (`0x40`) is a `boss` when an encounter the slice opens names it (the title, or a name its commas and lone "and"s divide it into) — else when its most health is at least 30% of the strongest POSTED hostile's (with none posted, none is) — and an `add` otherwise; anything else `friendly`; an NPC carries its creature id (the GUID's sixth field). POSTS: `HpHint.pos` (fields 14–17, x and y in hundredths of a yard, facing in ten-thousandths of a radian — exact) on the floor, health in tenths of a percent exactly as the file's one decimal formats it, power the hint's (`None` at max 0). The block sits at off12 behind a spell prefix — `SPELL_`, `RANGE_`, `DAMAGE_SHIELD`, every `_SUPPORT` and (v45) `DAMAGE_SPLIT`, whose block the parser read at off9 before, so never (R28's power and R23's sight of life read it now too) — else off9. EVENTS: a hit is any hostile spell-borne damage (`SPELL_`, `RANGE_`, `DAMAGE_SHIELD`, a building's; never environmental, never a melee swing) on a `Player-` from a source not OURS — neither `Player-` nor `Pet-`, nor a unit one of ours summoned (R22's fold: Spirit Link Totem's redistribution, its last lines a neutral `0xa28`, is no hit; a neutral NPC's spell still is) — kept only where the victim's own block places them on the floor, its `base` the suffix's unmitigated amount; a miss is any spell-borne `*_MISSED` on a player from such a source; a hostile cast is a `SPELL_CAST_START` / `_SUCCESS` by a unit flagged hostile by that line or an earlier one, never ours; a player's cast includes `SPELL_CAST_FAILED` (the logger's own); an aura row is a `SPELL_AURA_APPLIED` / `_REMOVED` / `_DOSE` on a player from the nil unit or a hostile one; a Feign Death is no death (R9) and a creature's `Unconscious` is down; the nil unit names no source and no target. PLACED: a player's landed cast of a placing spell (where they stood, its aim), a player's `SPELL_SUMMON` or `SPELL_CREATE` of one and the unit it made, a telling spell reaching a unit already numbered (an aura on it; a heal on it where the heal found it on the floor), a made unit dying or destroyed. MARKERS: those standing on the instance map at t 0, then each placement there and each removal — a placement on another map of one standing there is its removal (one object). HEAD: the encounter the slice opens with and its END (kill or wipe, its own fight time), else the run's key and its END; the instance map; the zone; the start in UTC and its local date. STORE: written for every Encounter and Key card (wipes included, an aborted one never) — on import and regrade beside the meter, after a live close as a cut alone once the history thread is idle — while the fight holds a REPLAY SLOT: the protected set (kills and timed keys kept whole, progression wipes, pins, the fastest kill, the owner's bests, the annotated) all season, else the newest `history_keep_details_per_encounter` of its group; past `history_replay_mb` the oldest unprotected go first, never a protected one; the rewrite queue cuts a slot's missing or older-format file (never a newer; at the cap only a fight newer than the oldest unprotected replay held, which the trim then takes), and a replay landing after its card is broadcast as `HistoryChanged`; answered by `GetReplay`, exported offline by `wowdps history replay-export` as the seven files a replay reads (`proto::replay::csv`, `raid.csv` from the details tier's R25 series). FIXTURES: `replay.txt` and check.awk's R29 block — every gated fixture's TSV carries each closed boss pull's `replay_*` rows (units, posts, floor, every event and placed kind, markers; posts per player). The DIFFERENCES from the extractor's text cutter the replay spike's pulls were cut with are listed in `core::replay`'s module doc (numbers read by the parser; names and hostility from modeled lines; a player named by the log, specced from the seeds or R8; Feign Death no death; ranged and shield damage as hits; no power at max 0; a clock that crosses midnight; the nil unit never a unit; a marker on one map at a time; a hit's source never one of ours).

## Where it lives

The cut is [`wowdps-core`](../crates/core.md)'s `replay` module (its module doc
lists every deliberate difference from the extractor's old text cutter); the
types are [`wowdps-model`](../crates/model.md)'s `replay` (`Cut`, `Unit`,
`Post`, `Event`, `Placed`, `Marker`, named as the files' columns); the tier's
binary format and the seven files a replay reads are
[`wowdps-proto`](../crates/proto.md)'s `replay` and `replay::csv`;
[`wowdps-daemon`](../crates/daemon.md) implements `PlacedTable` over
[`wowdps-encounter-rubric`](../crates/encounter-rubric.md)'s generated `placed`
table, writes `replay/<id>.bin` through the history store's import and rewrite
paths and answers `GetReplay`; [`wowdps-history`](../crates/history.md)'s
`replay-export` writes the files offline and `stats` counts the tier. The
parser reads the advanced block's fields 14–18 for it (and `DAMAGE_SPLIT`'s
block, which [R28](r28.md)'s power and [R23](r23.md)'s sight of life now read
too); the lines it alone reads parse as passive events no meter ledger reads,
so [R4](r4.md)'s segmentation and the scanner's lockstep are untouched, and a
creature's fall unconscious is down where [R9](r9.md) keeps a Feign Death no
death. The raid's damage each second is [R25](r25.md)'s, never stored again.
Gated by check.awk's R29 rows on every fixture, hand-worked in
[replay.txt](../fixtures/replay.md), and by `tests/replay.rs`'s lazy = full
cut; the decisions are
[The Replay Tier Lives In The History Store](../decisions/replay-tier-in-the-store.md)
and, for what it keeps, [A Progression Wipe Is Kept Whole](../decisions/progression-kept-whole.md);
the plan it builds on is
[A Fight Replay Captured Once](../decisions/fight-replay-captured-once.md).
