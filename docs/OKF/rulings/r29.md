**The call:** Every boss pull (an Encounter segment; never an arena) and every keystone run (a keyed visit's Σ) is CUT from its parsed lines into the history store's REPLAY tier (`core::replay::cut` → `model::replay::Cut` → `proto::replay`, `replay/<fight id>.bin`): its units numbered in order of first sight; every advanced block that places a player, creature or vehicle, on WHICHEVER floor it names, as a POST (x, y, facing, health share, power and — tier format 2 — its floor, the UiMap id; — format 3 — its shields and the last reading of every pool kind its unit's posts have named; — format 5 — each read as the game's own bar shows it: a cast's success after its cost, and a pool an energize fills that the target's block does not name raised by the gain), so a keystone run follows the party from level to level; the cut's FLOOR, what a boss pull is drawn on, is the UiMap id the players were posted on most, a tie to the lower id; the EVENT rows (a player's death, an NPC's death or fall unconscious, a rez; a hostile unit's cast begun or landed and a player's interrupt of it; a player's cast begun, landed or failed; a hostile spell's hit on a player where it found them, or its miss; a debuff from a hostile unit or from no one going on a player, coming off, changing stacks; — format 2 — every ENCOUNTER_START and ENCOUNTER_END the slice holds as a BOSS row: `boss_engaged`, `boss_killed` or `boss_wiped`, the encounter's id and name, no unit; and — format 3 — every debuff a player puts on an NPC, and the BUFF rows: `buff_applied`, `buff_removed`, `buff_dose` for a buff on a player that matters to the fight — one R18's curated table names (its support buffs aside) or one whose source is not ours — its removal or dose only once the cut saw it applied, save — format 4 — R18's segment-start rule: a debuff a player put on an NPC, or a buff R18 names, first seen by a refresh, a dose or a removal opens at the cut's start); what players PLACED (the generated table `proto::placed`); the WORLD MARKERS standing on the instance map at the start and each change. Passive: it moves no meter number and opens no segment.

**A conflicting change would:** Number units by anything but first sight (every stored file's ids would shift), drop a post off the main floor (a keystone run would lose its other levels) or keep a position the block did not give, keep a cast's pool from before it paid, guess a pool between two lines that say nothing of it (a regenerating mana bar) or let an energize open a continuous pool no block named, store the raid's damage per second a second time (R25 answers it), keep an NPC's buff that was up before the pull began (its removal or dose with no application in the cut), drop a defensive that was (R18 counts it from the pull's start), or let a cut's line open a segment or seed the meter and break the scanner's lockstep.

## Detail

ONE READING: every line through `parser::parse_line` once (`cut_and_meter` feeds the same parse to the meter on an import), the SEEDS for state — the latest COMBATANT_INFO spec per player, the zone, the keystone, the markers standing per map (a marker number is one object: a placement moves it to its map, off any other; a removal takes it off every map) — and the SLICE for the rest, its first line t 0 (ENCOUNTER_START, or a run's first line) and then read as every other line. The index's seeds carry every line this reads from before a slice (COMBATANT_INFO, ZONE_CHANGE, CHALLENGE_MODE, and since v45 every WORLD_MARKER line — index cache magic `\x13`), so a lazy cut equals the cut over every line before it (`tests/replay.rs`). UNITS: numbered where first seen, line by line, the line's post before its event row (an event numbers its unit, then its source, then its target), the units only a placed row names after all others. A `Player-` unit is `player`, class and spec from COMBATANT_INFO, else R8 over the slice (the meter's evidence: damage, healing, an absorb's shield, an interrupt, an aura applied, a dispel; the first class wins, a spec only with it), named by the log, `you` = the owner's GUID (the card's owner when stored); a unit some modeled line flagged hostile (`0x40`) is a `boss` when an encounter the slice opens names it (the title, or a name its commas and lone "and"s divide it into) — else when its most health is at least 30% of the strongest POSTED hostile's (with none posted, none is) — and an `add` otherwise; anything else `friendly`; an NPC carries its creature id (the GUID's sixth field). POSTS: `HpHint.pos` (fields 14–17, x and y in hundredths of a yard, facing in ten-thousandths of a radian — exact) on any floor, its UiMap id kept with it (`Post::map_id`; `Cut::maps` counts each floor's posts and players' posts, the FLOOR the most players'), health in tenths of a percent exactly as the file's one decimal formats it, power the hint's (`None` at max 0) — the first entry of a cast's `a|b` lists — and (format 3) `Post::absorb`, the hint's field 9 (the unit's shields in total), and `Post::pools`, the LAST reading of every pool kind the unit's posts have named so far, this one's in — every entry of the hint's `a|b` lists (`HpHint::pools`), each with its max above 0 and a power type below 64 (`model::replay::POOL_KINDS`; the parser reads a type at or above it as no pool) — by kind, one per kind, at most eight kinds (`MAX_POOLS`: a new kind past them is left out) (`model::replay::set_pool`); a kind never named is absent, and a line that posts nothing names nothing (pools grow from posts alone). FORMAT 5's READINGS: every current is the parser's, the block's less its offset 13 (the cast's cost, its own entry of the `a|b` list, never below 0): a `SPELL_CAST_SUCCESS` reports its caster before the cast pays (a Demonology warlock's Hand of Gul'dan, `7,50,50,30`, leaves 20 tenths of a shard); and a `SPELL_ENERGIZE` or `SPELL_PERIODIC_ENERGIZE` whose block posts its destination and names no reading of the power type its suffix fills (a warlock's shards, a paladin's holy power, a rogue's combo points gain on lines whose block names mana or energy; a block naming the type already reads after the gain and is taken as it stands) sets that kind to the unit's last reading of it (0 with none) plus the gain in the block's units — the suffix's amount × 10 for rage, runic power, soul shards and astral power (types 1, 6, 7, 8: the block counts tenths, the suffix whole ones, `1.0000` shard of `maxPower` 50), × 100 for insanity (13), × 1 for every other — at most the max (the suffix's, else the last reading's), and to that max outright when the suffix's overcap is above 0 (the gain filled the pool); with no max known it sets nothing. With no reading of that kind before it, an energize STARTS the pool (from 0) only when it is a COUNTED class resource, its max (the suffix's, in the block's units) below 100 (`COUNTED_POOL_MAX`: soul shards 50, holy power 5, combo points 5–7, chi 5–6, arcane charges 4, essence 5–6, runes 6 — named by a block only on a spender's line, empty out of combat); a CONTINUOUS pool (energy at 100 or more, focus, fury, maelstrom, rage, astral power, runic power, insanity, mana) no block has named is left alone, since spent on lines that never name it (a Restoration shaman's Mana Spring on an elemental shaman, whose blocks name maelstrom) it would only climb; once a block names it, its energizes advance it. A `SPELL_DRAIN` or `SPELL_LEECH` moves nothing (the parser models neither), and nothing between two lines is inferred. The block sits at off12 behind a spell prefix — `SPELL_`, `RANGE_`, `DAMAGE_SHIELD`, every `_SUPPORT` and (v45) `DAMAGE_SPLIT`, whose block the parser read at off9 before, so never (R28's power and R23's sight of life read it now too) — else off9. EVENTS: a hit is any hostile spell-borne damage (`SPELL_`, `RANGE_`, `DAMAGE_SHIELD`, a building's; never environmental, never a melee swing) on a `Player-` from a source not OURS — neither `Player-` nor `Pet-`, nor a unit one of ours summoned (R22's fold: Spirit Link Totem's redistribution, its last lines a neutral `0xa28`, is no hit; a neutral NPC's spell still is) — kept only where the victim's own block places them, on whatever floor (the victim's post at that moment carries it), its `base` the suffix's unmitigated amount; a miss is any spell-borne `*_MISSED` on a player from such a source; a hostile cast is a `SPELL_CAST_START` / `_SUCCESS` by a unit flagged hostile by that line or an earlier one, never ours; a player's cast includes `SPELL_CAST_FAILED` (the logger's own); a DEBUFF row is a `SPELL_AURA_APPLIED` / `_REMOVED` / `_DOSE` whose aura word is not `BUFF`, on a player from the nil unit or a hostile one, or (format 3) on a `Creature-` or `Vehicle-` unit from a `Player-` (every one, its `unit` the NPC, its `src` the player: what the player pressed, a triggered spell whose cast the log never writes included; a pet's, a guardian's or a totem's is none, never credited to its owner); a BUFF row (format 3: `buff_applied`, `buff_removed`, `buff_dose`, the debuff rows' columns) is the same lines with the word `BUFF` on a `Player-` whose spell R18's generated `role_spells` table names as anything but a `SupportBuff` (a defensive, a tank's mitigation, an external, a major or a healing cooldown — its caster in `src`) or whose source is not OURS (an NPC's, the nil unit's: an empowerment, a mark) — a long buff from before the pull (stamina, a flask) neither rule names is never a row; a buff from a hostile unit is a buff row, never a debuff row. AN AURA UP BEFORE THE CUT (format 4, R18's segment-start rule): a player's debuff on an NPC, or a buff R18's table names, keyed (unit, spell, source), whose first line in the slice is a `SPELL_AURA_REFRESH`, a dose or a removal — no application seen — was up when the slice began: an applied row at t 0 opens it, once per key, with that line's source, and the dose or removal is kept; a dose or removal of a key the slice has seen with nothing up is dropped, and a refresh is never a row of its own. Any other buff (an NPC's R18 does not name) keeps its removal or dose only once the slice applied it. The rows stand in time order (the opened row is found at its line, its units numbered there, and sorted to t 0 after the first lines' rows). Where the cut parts from R18: a key run is one slice, so its t 0 is the run's start where R18 opens at each member segment's start, and a span crossing a segment seam is one span where R18 has two; a pull's spans and R18's agree to the millisecond. A dose counts as a first sight too (R18 reads no dose); a Feign Death is no death (R9) and a creature's `Unconscious` is down; the nil unit names no source and no target; a BOSS row is every ENCOUNTER_START (`boss_engaged`) and ENCOUNTER_END (`boss_killed` on success, else `boss_wiped`) in the slice, whatever encounter it names, its `spell_id` and `spell` the encounter's id and name and its unit none (`Event::unit` is `None` on a boss row alone) — a boss pull's own two at t 0 and its end, a keystone run's a pair per boss at the card's `bosses` times. PLACED: a player's landed cast of a placing spell (where they stood, on whatever floor, its aim), a player's `SPELL_SUMMON` or `SPELL_CREATE` of one and the unit it made, a telling spell reaching a unit already numbered (an aura on it; a heal on it where the heal found it), a made unit dying or destroyed. MARKERS: those standing on the instance map at t 0, then each placement there and each removal — a placement on another map of one standing there is its removal (one object). HEAD: the encounter the slice opens with and its END (kill or wipe, its own fight time), else the run's key and its END; the key the slice is inside (a run's own, a pull's inside one: its name, level and — format 6 — affixes, R10's, as its START listed them; a run the log joined mid-way names its END's level and no affixes); the instance map; the zone; the start in UTC and its local date. STORE: written for every Encounter and Key card (wipes included, an aborted one never) — on import and regrade beside the meter, after a live close as a cut alone once the history thread is idle — while the fight holds a REPLAY SLOT: the protected set (kills and timed keys kept whole, progression wipes, pins, the fastest kill, the owner's bests, the annotated) all season, else the newest `history_keep_details_per_encounter` of its group; past `history_replay_mb` the oldest unprotected go first, never a protected one; the rewrite queue cuts a slot's missing or older-format file (never a newer; at the cap only a fight newer than the oldest unprotected replay held, which the trim then takes), and a replay landing after its card is broadcast as `HistoryChanged`; answered by `GetReplay`, exported offline by `wowdps history replay-export` as the seven files a replay reads (`proto::replay::csv`, `raid.csv` from the details tier's R25 series; `tracks.csv` ends each post with its `map_id` and (format 3) its `absorb` and `pools` (`kind:current:max`, `|` between; format 5: `power` and every pool read as the game's bar, a cast's after its cost and a pool an energize filled raised by its gain, in the block's units), `events.csv` writes the boss rows in its eight-column shape, `unit` empty, and the buff rows as it writes the debuff rows; format 6: `pull.txt` says `affixes = 10,9,147`, before `map`, when the head's key names some). TIER FORMAT 2 (`proto::replay::FORMAT`): format 1's sections, each post's floor in a MAPS section (the floors as a table, then only the units whose track leaves the head's floor, each change of floor at its post index — a pull on one floor pays four bytes) and the boss rows (event kinds 13–15, `has` bit 5 for a row naming no unit); a format-1 file still reads (every post on its head's floor, no boss row), and being older is recut from its log by the rewrite queue — no regrade. TIER FORMAT 3 (`proto::replay::FORMAT` 3, `OLDEST` 1): format 2's sections, its posts byte for byte, an ABSORB section 10 (each track's changes of shield at their post indices, a pull with none one byte) and a POOLS section 11 (only the posts whose pools are not their last's with their own pool set in: a cast's second pool), and the buff rows (event kinds 16–18); every format reads `Post::pools` by that same rule, so a format-2 file reads with its posts' own pools and no shield, and being older is recut from its log by the rewrite queue — no regrade, no wire change (`GetReplay` carries the bytes opaquely). TIER FORMAT 4 (`FORMAT` 4): format 3's codec byte for byte but the head's number, which moved with the cut's aura rules (the segment-start rule above) — the rewrite queue knows a file by its number alone, so it recuts every format-1, 2 and 3 file from its log. A decoder refuses a power type at or above `POOL_KINDS` and a pools list longer than `MAX_POOLS` (before allocating it), so a post's pools stay a few readings whatever a file says; `encode` writes each post's pools tidied (`model::replay::next_pools`: sorted, one per kind, bounded; a post that lists none grown from its own pool) and a power type no reader takes as none, so what it writes always decodes. TIER FORMAT 5 (`FORMAT` 5, 2026-10-11): format 4's codec byte for byte but the head's number, which moved with FORMAT 5's READINGS above (a cast's pools after its cost, an energize's gain on the pool it fills; the pools section lists the posts an energize set as it lists a cast's second pool) — every stored file's `power` and `pools` move, so the rewrite queue recuts every format-1 to 4 file from its log while idle; no regrade for the tier, no wire change (`GetReplay` carries the bytes opaquely). TIER FORMAT 6 (`FORMAT` 6, 2026-10-17, v47): format 5's sections byte for byte but the head, whose key adds its affixes after its level (a varint count, then each id a varint; a key with none one byte more) — a format-1 to 5 file still reads, its key with no affixes, and being older is recut from its log by the rewrite queue while idle, so every run kept in a replay slot gets its affixes back while its log survives; no regrade for the tier, no wire change. ## Where it lives

The cut is [`wowdps-core`](../crates/core.md)'s `replay` module (its module doc
lists every deliberate difference from the extractor's old text cutter); the
types are [`wowdps-model`](../crates/model.md)'s `replay` (`Cut`, `Unit`,
`Post` with its `map_id` and (format 3) its `absorb` and `pools`, `Event`,
`Placed`, `Marker`, named as the files' columns, `MapPosts`, what `Cut::maps`
counts, and `set_pool`, the one rule a post's pools grow by); the buff rows'
curated spells are [R18](r18.md)'s `role_spells` table; the tier's binary
format (format 3 since the shields, every pool kind and the aura rows a
followed unit's HUD reads; format 2 since every floor and the boss rows;
formats 1 and 2 still read) and the seven files a replay reads are [`wowdps-proto`](../crates/proto.md)'s
`replay` and `replay::csv`;
[`wowdps-daemon`](../crates/daemon.md) implements `PlacedTable` over
[`wowdps-proto`](../crates/proto.md)'s generated `placed` table
(`proto::placed`, [gen-placed-spells](../tools/gen-placed-spells.md)), writes `replay/<id>.bin` through the history store's import and rewrite
paths (the rewrite queue recutting an older format) and answers `GetReplay`;
[`wowdps-history`](../crates/history.md)'s `replay-export` writes the files
offline and `stats` counts the tier, by format too. The
parser reads the advanced block's fields 14–18 for it (and `DAMAGE_SPLIT`'s
block, which [R28](r28.md)'s power and [R23](r23.md)'s sight of life now read
too); the lines it alone reads parse as passive events no meter ledger reads,
so [R4](r4.md)'s segmentation and the scanner's lockstep are untouched, and a
creature's fall unconscious is down where [R9](r9.md) keeps a Feign Death no
death. The raid's damage each second is [R25](r25.md)'s, never stored again.
A run's head carries its key's affixes since format 6
([Keep A Keystone Run's Affixes](../decisions/keep-keystone-affixes.md)).
Gated by check.awk's R29 rows on every fixture, hand-worked in
[replay.txt](../fixtures/replay.md) (its keystone run's rows under `K1`), and
by `tests/replay.rs`'s lazy = full cut, pulls and runs alike; the decisions are
[The Replay Tier Lives In The History Store](../decisions/replay-tier-in-the-store.md)
