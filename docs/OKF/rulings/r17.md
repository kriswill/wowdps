**The call:** Every damage event whose destination is a player or pet (pets fold onto owners, R4) is recorded a second time on the DESTINATION as `View::Taken`: amount = R1's `amount + absorbed` (the log's amount is post-block, so blocked is NOT added — per segment Σ every actor's Damage by_target over friendly names + Σ R22 `self_harm` = Σ Taken rows + Σ `stagger_ticked`, exactly), extra = absorbed, by-spell = taken by ability, by-target = taken by ATTACKER NAME (R5's pet-by-name precedent; never a guid). A `*_MISSED` line has no damage twin: it is count 1 / amount 0 on the Taken row and its drill rows (a player who was only dodged still earns a row — Taken lists on count > 0), and its BLOCK amount or ABSORB amountMissed is PREVENTED damage in the per-player `Mitigation` record (partial absorbs + blocks + full absorbs + blocks = `mitigated`; `mitigated_pct` = mitigated / (taken + full-miss amounts); dodge/parry/miss carry no amount and are counts only). SPELL_ABSORBED is never read by Taken (R3). STAGGER: the `NON_HEALING_ABSORBS` amount consumed on a player is `stagger`, a subset of absorbed (R3's premise), never added again; the self-sourced Stagger ticks (124255, src = dst) that re-deal it are EXCLUDED from Taken and tallied as `stagger_ticked` (their amount + absorbed, exactly what Taken would have recorded — so the identity above is exact; R22 amends R1 — a tick is self-harm, held off the Damage row and tallied as `self_harm` too). ENVIRONMENTAL_DAMAGE is labeled by its envType; a nil source is named "Environment". The mitigation map is keyed by raw destination guid and folds at read time. Segment-local; never opens, extends or splits a segment (the scanner ignores *_MISSED and the NON_HEALING_ABSORBS SPELL_ABSORBED; the meter records either only into an already-open segment that is NOT past the R4 trash gap — it mirrors `ensure_combat`'s split predicate without acting on it — and never touches last_ms). Consequently a miss logged more than 60 s after a Trash segment's last combat lands in no segment, and a stagger absorb logged before the pull's first hit (the game logs SPELL_ABSORBED just before the hit it shields: after an ENCOUNTER_END, a lull, or at the log's start) is NOT attributed — the pull's byte range starts at the hit, so a lazy load could never see that line, and full replay must agree with it.

**A conflicting change would:** Break the taken = dealt identity, count a staggered hit twice, or open a segment from a miss and break scanner/meter lockstep.
