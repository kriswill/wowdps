## Rules

- Membership is discovered by `tools/extract/src/absorbgen.rs`: every spell id with
  a `SpellEffect` row whose `EffectAura == 69` (SCHOOL_ABSORB) on some difficulty.
  Nothing is curated; the name column comes from `SpellName` for review only.
- The fixtures' shields — Power Word: Shield 17 — must be in the result or the
  generator fails, naming the id.
- The census `tools/forever/absorb-spells-census.csv` (`tools/census-absorb-spells.sh
  <log>...`: every `SPELL_ABSORBED`'s absorb spell — field n−5 of n — counted per
  (id, name) per log) is evidence, not a gate: its counts print beside each entry,
  and every census id the table lacks is listed under *Census ids outside the
  table*. Those are the shields the R20 ledger opens as `known = false` applied on
  their first `SPELL_ABSORBED` — their consumed amounts still count, only the
  applied/wasted split is unknown.
- The engine (CONTRACT.md R20) admits a Buff aura into the shield ledger only when
  `is_absorb_spell(id)` holds — never on the aura trailer alone.
