## Rules

- Membership is curated in `tools/extract/src/spangen.rs` as `(name, kind, class)`;
  the client expands each name to its ids. An id joins when `SpellName` carries
  the name, `SpellEffect` gives it an aura a player can wear (APPLY_AURA, or an
  area aura on the caster's party or raid) and no rune passive's
  OVERRIDE_ACTIONBAR_SPELLS aura, `SpellMisc` does not mark it PASSIVE (a set
  bonus or a talent of the name), and `SpellClassOptions` puts it in the entry's
  own class's spell family (`its class's family, an aura`), or it is in no class's
  family and a same-named id of the entry's triggers it (`triggered by N`) or names
  it in its own text (`named by N`): a cast that is a dummy and the buff it gives.
  Another class's spell of the name never joins. A name no id passes fails the
  build.
- The committed census `tools/forever/role-spells-census.csv`
  (`tools/census-role-spells.sh <log>...`: every `SPELL_AURA_APPLIED` BUFF on a
  `Player-` guid, counted per (id, name) per log) must show one of a name's ids
  applied, under that name, unless the entry carries a `census_exempt` reason,
  printed below in place of the counts (`exempt: …`).
- What a WAIVED entry's ids are known to be: each carries the name and an aura in
  a class's family, or a tie the client records to an id that does. What they
  are NOT known to be: the id the log applies. No committed log shows one on a
  player, so a buff the client ties to nothing would be missed, and the first
  census to hold one settles it.
- The engine (CONTRACT.md R18) opens a span keyed by the target with the caster as
  `src` on a Buff apply/refresh of a curated id, and closes it on the removal.
