## Rules

- Membership is curated in `tools/extract/src/rolegen.rs` as `(aura id, name, kind)`
  — the id is the AURA the log names on `SPELL_AURA_APPLIED`, never the cast's
  (Blur is 212800, not 198589; Metamorphosis 162264, not 191427).
- Every entry is proven against the client: `SpellName.Name_lang` must equal the
  curated name, and `SpellEffect` must hold an `Effect == 6` (APPLY_AURA) row for
  the id on some difficulty. Either miss fails the build, naming the id.
- Every entry must be exercised by the committed census `tools/role-spells-census.csv`
  (`tools/census-role-spells.sh <log>...`: every `SPELL_AURA_APPLIED` whose target
  is a `Player-` guid and whose aura type is `BUFF`, counted per (id, name) per
  log), under the same name. A curated id nobody has seen on a player is a build
  failure — the coach requests an id, the census proves it, then it ships — unless
  the entry carries a `census_exempt` reason (`Curated::exempt`): name and
  APPLY_AURA are still proven, and the reason is printed below in place of the
  counts (`exempt: …`) so the waiver stands beside the evidence it replaces.
- No class/spec gate: an external lands on its target regardless of class, and
  nothing reads one. Kinds: `active_mitigation` (a tank's rotational mitigation),
  `defensive` (a personal damage-reduction cooldown), `external` (a buff cast on
  someone else), `support_buff` (a buff whose value is the target's output),
  `cooldown` (a major offensive cooldown's own buff),
  `healing_cooldown` (a major healing cooldown's own buff — v34; a hybrid's
  window such as Avenging Wrath stays `cooldown`).
- The engine (CONTRACT.md R18) opens a span keyed by the target with the caster as
  `src` on a Buff apply/refresh of a curated id, and closes it on the removal.
