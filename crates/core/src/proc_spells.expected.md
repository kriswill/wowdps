## Rules

- Membership is curated in `tools/extract/src/procgen.rs` as `(proc id, proc name,
  driver id, driver name, spec)`; the tree matches the driver's row by name.
- The install proves each entry: `SpellName` carries both names, and the client's
  own text ties them — the proc's description (through `$@spelldesc`), or one it
  references, names the driver; or the text references the driver's id; or a
  `SpellEffect` of the driver triggers the proc (directly or through one spell).
- The logs prove it too: `tools/proc-spells-census.csv`
  (`tools/census-proc-spells.sh <log>...`) counts the proc's direct onsets within
  1.5 s after the same player's driver, and its hits on a target carrying the
  player's driver aura; the better share must reach 90%, unless the entry
  carries a `census_exempt` reason, printed in place of the verdict.
- A proc with two drivers has no entry: a row nests under one spell.
